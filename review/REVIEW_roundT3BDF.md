=== B. Noise theorem N / N-G ===
Findings
B1 MAJOR. Certified noise range is tiny. I recomputed st_crit: s_crit/A_min = 0.02 (K=1), 4e-4 (K=2), 7.6e-5 (K=3) at N=181, p=0.05; over my 96 certified pairs the gauge e0 at 0.97 t_cert is 1.4e-4 .. 3.4e-2 (median 2e-3). The certified scale is proportional to the runner-up margin (e0 <= c mu/tau_K, c in 0.10-0.50). Fix: print these numbers next to the theorem; call it a small-noise theorem.
B2 MAJOR. Conditional/computer-assisted status. S is evaluated on double-precision limit data; the ball run covers the inequalities only (data widened 1e-9), not an enclosure of the path. Hypotheses: limit object (not the trained network), continuum Gram, n=1, equal walls, finite dictionary. Fix: wording below.
B3 MINOR. "(ii) the block is the K-unit least-squares fit" is proved only as "the unique critical point of the K-unit fit in the box D*"; global minimality is not proved. Fix: reword.
B4 MINOR. The entering orbit (entrant leaves v=0; "the orbit lies in the box from some time on") is inherited from Prop. S-A's unproved paragraph; the repo's validation (Part V) uses the least-squares polish, not the flow. My flow-based runs close this empirically only (v0 = 1e-5).
B5 MINOR. "O(e), O(1/e), O(e^2)" are meaningful only inside S. The window bound vanishes at the boundary of S (ratio 1e-15 at t_cert); at 0.5 t_cert the median W_lo^z/actual is 0.035. Rem is never above 5% of its bound; the block bound is at most 0.69 attained. Fix: say "explicit lower bound, sharp to first order as e->0", not "~1/e" unqualified.
B6 MINOR. Monotonicity of S "by inspection" is not needed: every proof step uses only upper bounds on the gauges. State the theorem with upper bounds E and drop the monotonicity claim (my 504 random gauge pairs: 0 violations).
Answers to the attack questions
- "No other unit before the K-th" for ALL dictionary kernels: proved. The competitor condition M_{k,j}<1 is checked for every inactive j in D at every stage (system(), lines 352-362); e0 is a sup over N_r and D; far kernels enter through eps_j = e0, in-box kernels through Gbar ~ 1. I re-derived the line comparison, the eta recursion and the ebar update: correct.
- Box invariance/uniqueness with noise in the couplings: proved, not assumed (E_v+e0, E_c+e1, own Hessian block [[0,-g1],[-g1,-v g2]]). I re-derived the own block (t = a v psi'(d)) and the faces; box_noise() matches.
- Window with a noise-changed block: valid. The rate is <xi,(I-P_T)phi_j> - <rho,phi_j> with ||rho|| <= Rem; I re-derived Lemma N5 (M_2, L_J, ||E^T xi||, the identity for (I-P_T)q + J*H*^-1 E^T(...)): correct.
- Sub-Gaussian net: correct (net of mesh r/M <= 1/(4 sqrt N), Taylor chain g_j -> g_{j+1}, closed by Cauchy-Schwarz and the Hsu-Kakade-Zhang tail; union over nev = 231-561 events).
- Are 0 violations informative: yes for entry times and window (0.97-0.998 of the bound attained), no for Rem and the block distance.

=== D. Readout theorems ===
Findings
D1 MINOR. "Sharp" is true only for the exact form |w| <= s^p[mu^-q - (2-mu)^-q] (attained iff |eta| reaches (1-mu)lam). The displayed (eps b)^(p/2) mu^(-p/(p-2)) is never attained: ratio 1-(mu/(2-mu))^q = 0.963 at mu=0.5, 0.70 at mu=0.8 (p=3), 0 at mu=1. Fix: "sharp as mu->0" or print the exact form.
D2 MAJOR. Hypotheses of Thms 2-3 are on the ACTUAL dual eta = int g, not the path's. They do not give "depth 2 reaches the path only if mu > ln(1/theta)/ln(1/eps)" for the network; that remains an observed rule (the note agrees). Fix: say so in the main text.
D3 MAJOR. Fixed-centre limit (Cor. 5): the proved part is elementary (epi-convergence of one-dimensional potentials, recovery sequence (1-delta)zeta); the convergence is entirely the cited step. I checked the cited hypotheses are met: convex lsc proper potentials (W increasing; barrier +inf at the wall), Mosco = epi in finite dimension, zeta(0)=0 in the domains' closure. What it yields: duals converge uniformly on compact rescaled-time intervals and Phi w in L2_loc. It yields no rate, no pointwise convergence of w, nothing for moving centres. "At log rate" and "eps^1/2" are numerical. Fix: cite the theorems by number, label the rates "observed", cite Pesme-Flammarion for the depth-2 limit.
D4 MAJOR. The "explanation of 0.26" is post hoc and unfalsifiable as written:
 (a) the attenuation formula is an algebraic identity of the sample: the code sets b_ = a_ - 0.5 log10(eps*_observed) (theory3d_readouts.py, line ~875), so it reproduces any slope;
 (b) 0.239 is the D3 regression slope on a truncated scan (margin < 0.03; 42 of 133 right-censored), while the paper's 0.26 is a two-point exponent of the largest mismatched margin for the paper's unit. Like-for-like numbers: D3 0.40; paper's-unit scan slope 0.10;
 (c) bootstrap 5-95% intervals 0.09-0.71 (D3) and 0.15-0.55 (paper's unit) contain both 0.26 and 0.5; the model's largest flipped margin is constant (0.0112) from 3e-8 to 1e-9, i.e. one target;
 (d) the model variant was chosen among three after seeing the counts (note, gap 5); precision 60%; it over-predicts the tie family's critical eps by 2.2-3.1;
 (e) on the tie family the scalar formation margin theta^(-1/3) eps^(1/2) (theta = 0.008) predicts eps* within 0.55-0.74 of the observed value (my arithmetic on 3 members), better than the overtaking model, so the family does not discriminate the two mechanisms. In my runs the "flip" is the runner-up reaching w ~ 0.010 just above theta.
 Fix: keep only "at fixed geometry the critical margin scales as eps^0.53 (tie family); per-target constants spread over two orders of magnitude, so pooled statistics have no universal exponent; 0.26 is descriptive".
Verified (how)
- Closed forms with u AND v, own ODE, drive with 7-16 sign changes, b != 1, ell != 1, |eta|/lam up to 0.97: p=1..5, 15 cases, relative error <= 1e-7 (<= 1e-9 for p>=2).
- Repo flow (make_rhs) with moving centres and unequal masses, eta integrated alongside: |w - W(int g)| relative <= 4e-10 (D2), 2e-7 (D3), 1e-6 (D4), 2e-6 (D5), 1e-13 (D1); 3-8 of 9 duals non-monotone. Conventions match the paper (rate ell on each of u, v; s^2 = eps b).
- Fixed-centre limit against my own weighted-l1 ISS (7 kernels, unequal masses, 3-6 events): D3 sup dual error 0.095, 0.032, 0.010, 0.0033 for eps 1e-3..1e-6 (slope 0.49) with walls b^-1/2; unweighted walls are off by 0.43. D4 slope 0.9 with walls b^-1. D2: 0.155, 0.078, 0.039, 0.020 for eps 1e-6, 1e-12, 1e-24, 1e-48 (exactly 1/ln(1/eps)), unweighted walls; b^-1/2 walls off by 0.42.
- Tie family (D3): 3 of the 8 brackets reproduced with my own flows (no flip at lo/1.15, flip at hi x 1.15); slope from these three 0.52.
Could not check: the 133-target scan, Parts F/G/H raw data, bootstrap, ball arithmetic, tests/mutants; the paper's own unit W^eps (cited lemma).
Statement the paper may use
"Lemma (exact unit). For gradient flow with rate ell on (u,v) from u=v=s, s^2 = eps b, and any continuous drive g(t) = <r phi(.;c(t))> (other units and the centre arbitrary), with eta = int_0^t g: depth 1: w = ell eta; depth 2: w = 2 eps b sinh(4 ell eta); depth p>=3: w = s^p[(1-eta/lam)^-q - (1+eta/lam)^-q], lam = s^-(p-2)/(ell p (p-2)), q = p/(p-2), for |eta| < lam. If |eta| <= (1-mu) lam on [0,T] then |w| <= s^p[mu^-q - (2-mu)^-q] <= (eps b)^(p/2) mu^(-p/(p-2)), the first with equality iff attained. Depth 2 at the fraction 1-mu of lam_2 = ln(1/(eps b))/(4 ell): sup|w| = (eps b)^mu - (eps b)^(2-mu). Depth 1 has no wall."
"Fixed centres: by Attouch's theorem and the Brezis-Pazy theorem (hypotheses verified: epi-convergence of the potentials), the rescaled duals converge uniformly on compact time intervals to the inverse scale space of sum_j b_j^-(p-2)/2 |w_j| (p>=3) and of sum_j |w_j| (p=2); the observed rates are eps^1/2 (p=3) and 1/ln(1/eps) (p=2)."
Must not claim: a sliding-limit theorem for D_p; that the scalar bound predicts the network's mismatches; that 0.26 is explained or derived; "no fitted parameter" without disclosing the post hoc model choice; the depth-2 rule as a theorem.

=== F. Optimizers and depth ===
Findings
F1 BLOCKER for the sentence "PROVED: (iii) global-norm GD / (iv) shared scalar second moment: same orbits, the order is kept". True only in continuous time, or lr << s. At practical steps the first normalised step moves every unit by about lr b_i g_i/||.||, erasing the small start; the run then follows a path with walls proportional to 1/(b_i |g_i(0)|).
 - Two units, coupled residual: NOT refuted. First entrant identical to the flow's in 0/182 differing cases (78 equal-mass, 104 unequal-mass random systems; Adam shared-v with beta1 = 0.9 and 0, lr 1e-3 and 1e-2, and global-norm GD); with long budgets entered sets equal on 39/39. Reason: after the big step the blow-up key is u_i g_i ~ b_i g_i^2, the same order as the flow's sqrt(b_i) g_i. But timing is not kept: T2/T1 = 1.07-1.12 in the flow against 11-35 with shared v.
 - Three units, EQUAL masses: counterexample. G = [[1,-0.4,0.81],[-0.4,1,-0.3],[0.81,-0.3,1]] (eigenvalues 0.18, 0.77, 2.05), c = (1, 0.3, 0.9), theta = 0.01. Flow order (1,2,3) with T = 332, 664, 696 (s=1e-3). Shared-v Adam (beta1 = 0.9 or 0, lr=1e-3): (1,3,2), T = 68, 5702, 3758. Global-norm GD: (1,3,2). At s=1e-4 unit 2 does not enter within 8000 shared-v steps. lr = s/50 restores (1,2,3).
 Fix: state (iii)/(iv) for the continuous-time limit; say "restores the formed set on the tested cases at lr >= s; the order of later entries can differ".
F2 MAJOR. F1b window theorem is vacuous. The constant 72.7 exceeds the GD spread g1/g2 whenever g1/g2 < 72.7. Observed Adam windows are 1.00-1.61 against GD 100 (drives 1..0.01). Default Adam (bias correction, eps_adam = 1e-8) is outside the hypothesis eps_a <= 3 g s^2 for g < 0.002 at eps = 1.7e-6, g < 0.19 at 1.7e-8, all g at 1.7e-10; yet the windows are 1.02, 1.16, 1.61. The v coordinate (decreasing gradients) and the paper's (alpha, nu) are not covered. Fix: keep Prop 2a (exact scale invariance, eps_adam = 0, frozen drive) as the theorem; report the window as numerics.
F3 MINOR. F1a hypotheses must be in the statement: fixed dictionary/Gram (trainable centres not covered), common start and step, s not a multiple of lr, |g_i(0)| > theta ||G_i||_1, and the escape phase only (up to the first crossing). "Exactly the same step" is an artefact of identical starts: with masses b in [0.14, 3.9] and s = 0.013 the steps spread 72-80 (lr 1e-3) and 736-790 (lr 1e-4), within the stated bound. After 20000 sign steps on my redundant dictionary 17 of 21 units stay above 0.009, so "only the path is wrong, not the limit point" holds for the full-rank pair only.
F4 MAJOR. F2b is a measure-zero statement and is false for Adam in practice. In the repo's KanToy (factorised second layer, pure small start, 3000 steps): centred target: stuck for GD and Adam(1e-8) as claimed; Adam with eps_adam = 1e-30 escapes from round-off (first gradient 1.5e-19, final rms 0.17). Target mean 1e-3 of the std: Adam default escapes and fits (rms 0.176, weights O(1)); GD stays at rms 1.0. So "exact equilibrium for GD, momentum and Adam" needs exactly zero coefficients in both layers, an exactly centred full-batch target, and gradients below eps_adam. Fix: state it as an escape-time law for GD (T ~ ln(s/a0)/(c* s^4), i.e. eps^-2 ln) and say Adam leaves at rate lr once any gradient exceeds eps_adam.
F5 MINOR. KAN numbers: 0.67/0.27/0.70 are 6 runs (3 development targets, 2 noise levels, seed 0; 30 edges), no intervals, and the share counts irrelevant edges (K=0) that every method gets right. GD hit the 12000-step cap in 15 of my 21 runs. Fix: report the rerun below and the share on K>0 edges.
