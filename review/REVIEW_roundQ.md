# REVIEW_roundQ: referee report on the pre-registered real-data mechanism test (mechreal)

Archived verbatim from the referee's hand-back (2026-11-10). The referee left the repository untouched; scratch code is outside it. Note: the referee's remark "the prompt's phrase has the sign wrong" refers to the coordinator's brief, which said "leave zero"; the paper wording uses "awake".

REFEREE Q: real-data mechanism test (mechreal). Verdict: the registration and the arithmetic are sound, every headline number reproduces, and no blocker invalidates a verdict. The danger is the wording: C, D, E and the "counts as well as F" sentence are easy to over-read, and the real-data row of Table 2 is still \PENDING (the paper has no mechreal text yet).

FINDINGS
1. MAJOR (wording; the prompt's phrase has the sign wrong). C and D do NOT "leave zero on nearly all units". 476 and 470 of 484 units are AWAKE (|w|>0.05; A has 10.2, F 7.5). Correct statement: depth 1 and default Adam leave nearly all units awake, so dormancy fails. Fix: reword.
2. MAJOR (O1, Adam). "D forms more units than A" is budget-dependent. At the registered 100000 steps, D minus A in K_hat is +69.1 [63.0, 74.3]. In the registered secondary readings it flips sign: best case -1.86 [-3.19,-0.42], equal-fit -2.69 [-4.06,-1.08], BIC -1.89 [-3.42,-0.14]. What is robust is that D's count error exceeds A's (+2.8 best case, +3.4 equal-fit, +3.2 BIC, +68 at budget) and that D is awake on about 470 of 484 units. D is converged in fit (median loss 12.6, about 0.003 of F's block loss) but not in K_hat: the test median creeps 69 to 75.5 between 50k and 100k steps. The 100000 budget is the fixed-budget reading, chosen after dev truth showed an early stop was degenerate; it makes D look worse than any tuned stop does. Fix: never quote "69 extra units" without "at the fixed budget".
3. MAJOR (C, depth 1). The 114 is not a budget artefact in sign or order: 24 of 36 runs hit 3600 s, but every unfinished run had count error >=100 at its last COMPLETED snapshot (0.15 tau30; per-window range 100 to 150). The unfinished state is only 0.46 to 0.96 of tau30, and amplitudes grow, so the budget understates C's error. On the 12 finished windows C minus A is 88.0 [57.5, 115.8]. But 114 is the error at A's physical time (depth 1 has no clock of its own; plan convention). Label-free BIC stop: C minus A is only +1.39 [0.81, 2.00]. Best case: C minus A is +1.44 (C count error 2.44). So the true statement is "depth 1 forms a dense solution and loses the count at the wall-clock of A; at an early or BIC stop it is 1.4 worse". Fix: report 114 with its convention and the BIC row.
4. MAJOR (E / O2). O2 FAILED follows from the registered rule: E minus A is 0.83 [0.36, 1.33], upper limit above 1; cluster bootstrap [0.21, 1.50]. The margin of 1.0 equals A's own error, and the point estimate (0.83) lies inside it, so "failed" means "not demonstrated within margin", not "E is far worse". E is a transient at the budget: loss 1420 at 100k against F 3650, median K_hat 3 at 50k and 5 at 100k. Equal-fit reading: 0.14 [-0.22, 0.50], within margin (registered secondary, not the verdict). O2b also failed (1.17 [0.47, 1.83]). Fix: say "shared-moment Adam counts about as well as A at equal fit; at the fixed budget it was not within the registered margin". Never write that O held.
5. MAJOR (P-R, R1 is fragile). Verdicts follow from the registered rules, but R1 (B minus A) is 0.47 [0.06, 0.86] by window bootstrap and [-0.14, 1.09] by patch bootstrap. Best case 0.36 [-0.03, 0.75]; dev showed no difference (1.50 vs 1.38). B's error (1.47) equals a trivial greedy lattice matching pursuit (1.47, see 7). R2 and R3 (depth 1) are robust. Fix: "P-R held, carried by depth 1; the depth-2 effect is 0.47 against 1.00 and is not significant when whole patches are resampled".
6. MAJOR (P-path is not a clean failure of the network). I reproduced 11/36 and 15/36 from the stored A snapshot at tau_mid and the stored polished blocks (own C1/C2 code). Post hoc: (a) the signed path (Definition 1) itself has negative units in 22 of 36 windows, and the network has negative formed units in the same 22. Where the signed path has none (14 windows) A forms the block 11/14 = 0.79, the harness's 0.75 to 0.88. Where it has negatives, A agrees with F 0/22 and with the signed path 4/22. (b) Dropping A's negative units against F gives 17/36 (0.47), and 18/36 with merging, so negatives explain about 6 of the 25 failures, not most. (c) The criterion uses the harness threshold min(0.01 max, 0.1 min |w_block|), median 0.064 f_lim (36% below 0.05), about 13 times below the counting theta of 0.85, so sub-count confusion units fail it. With both sides at theta 0.85 and merging: 18/36 against F, 21/36 (0.58) against the signed path. (d) Agreement rises with the gap tau_mid minus tau_K in units of sqrt(eps)=1e-4: gap >=3 gives 7/11 (F) and 8/11 (signed); gap 1 to 3 gives 2/19 and 5/19; median gap is 2.1, i.e. the asymptotic regime eps^(1/2) ln(1/eps) << tau is not reached at eps=1e-8 (dev: 0 and 1 of 8 at both 1e-6 and 1e-8). No variant reaches 0.60 in all windows. So "P-path FAILED" stands; "the network does not follow the path on real windows" is not established; write "not shown on real, confusion-limited windows; follows on 11 of 14 windows whose signed path has no negative unit (post hoc)".
7. MAJOR (what "count as well as F" means). A and F both 1.00 against mean K_true 4.19 (SD 2.39); A is unbiased (signed -0.11). Trivial baselines on the same 36 windows (my code, theta chosen on dev + devx only): constant 3 (the median, an oracle) 1.75; constant 6.5 (the dev mean) 3.03; lattice matched-filter peak count 1.75 (A minus it: -0.75 [-1.28,-0.25]); greedy lattice LS matching pursuit 1.47 (A minus it: -0.47 [-0.89,-0.06], patches [-0.78,-0.19]). So A and F are about 0.5 to 0.75 better than trivial, with K_hat correlated 0.85 with K_true. Equivalence to F is by construction: same stop and theta (tuned on F), K_hat equal on 28 of 36 windows, mean |K_A-K_F| 0.33, K_hat correlation 0.94. The equivalence is a statement about the network reproducing the path's count, not about counting accuracy. Count error depends strongly on the truth definition: if the flux limit is scaled by 0.7 or 1.4 the error rises from 1.00 to 1.94 / 1.75 (F 2.06 / 1.69); the interior edge (2.0 to 3.0 px) changes it by <=0.2. 21 of 36 windows have a source >=f_lim within 0.5 px of the interior boundary (mean 1.03 per window), the flux of sources outside the interior is 58 f_lim against 21 inside, and 7.8 sources >=f_lim lie beyond the window (up to 5 px pad). The truth count is a threshold count, not "the number of stars".
8. MINOR (registration, selection; no leakage found). Plan, freeze and code unchanged since 3d74392 (sha256 in the freeze matches the committed files; git log shows one commit per file). The dev raw at f165959 has no test keys. I reran select_windows('test') offline from the committed code and data: it gives the same 36 names and f_lim, so selection is deterministic and reproducible. Rule uses only truth geometry (2 to 12 counted sources, brightest <=50 f_lim, seeded permutation); no arm outcome enters. Conditions applied: none of the 8 test patches is M2 (ngc5466, 5904, 6535, 6838, 6934, 5272, 7078); the 3 failed-registration patches are excluded (that exclusion is post hoc in clusterdep, from earlier detector results, before this study). Selection conditions on K_true in [2,12], so empty or crowded windows (K=0, >12) are never scored and false alarms on empty windows cannot appear; Z_LIM=40 was chosen after seeing the dev star counts. Test windows come from the same pixels used by earlier path-as-fitter experiments (stated in the plan). Timing: commit 3d74392 20:02:44, freeze 20:02:31, test select 20:02:56 (in the raw, self-written). The plan text "Written 20:03Z" is rounded up and is later than both the commit and the selection; the commit time is the registration time (the plan hash is frozen, so do not edit it; state 20:02:44Z in the paper). Push time not externally verifiable.
9. MINOR (tuning fairness). kappa=30 and theta=0.85 were chosen from F alone on 24 dev candidates (M2, PSF 0.79 px; test PSF 0.82 to 1.31 px) and inherited by A to E, so A is tuned only through F. B to E best cases are tuned on dev truth and A is not, so those rows favour the rivals; A still wins them. Windows in one patch share PSF and noise, so the window bootstrap is optimistic; report patch intervals next to window intervals (8 patches, 7 clusters; ngc7078 gives 10 of 36 windows).

VERIFIED (own code, not the repo's tables): K_hat, count error, awake counts per arm at the frozen stop from raw (own merge/threshold, equals the report: A 1.00, B 1.47, C 114.19, D 69.00, E 1.83, F 1.00); own window bootstrap (40000, seed 777) and patch bootstrap give the same intervals as the report to 0.05 (B-A [0.06,0.86], patch [-0.14,1.09]; E-A [0.33,1.31]; A-F [-0.22,0.22]); O1 and R2/R3 hold, O2 and O2b fail at the margins 1.0 and 1.0, R1 holds by lower limit 0.06; P-path 11/36 and 15/36 recomputed independently, units above 0.05 mean 475.7 (C), 469.6 (D), 10.2 (A); selection rerun reproduces the windows; hashes match the freeze; tests/test_mechreal.py passes (37 passed in 24 s); repo untouched (git status clean).

COULD NOT CHECK: the real commit and push time (git dates are author-controlled); the HST truth itself and the F606W to r flux calibration (a colour term would shift the count threshold); the dev raw's agreement with the freeze beyond the hash; the 1e-10 and smaller eps behaviour on test windows (not run).

ALLOWED WORDING (use this): "On 36 held-out 20 x 20 px SDSS windows of 7 globular clusters (8 patches) with HST truth, the factorised network trained by plain gradient flow from eps = 1e-8 and stopped at the frozen time and threshold reached a mean count error of 1.00 against a mean true count of 4.19 (stars above a 40-sigma flux limit), the same as the computed path's 1.00 (difference 0.00 [-0.22, 0.22]); both beat a simple lattice matched-filter count (1.75) and a greedy lattice fit (1.47). Depth 1 and default Adam left about 470 of 484 units awake and lost the count at the registered clock and budget (114 and 69 count errors, depth 1 under a time-budget caveat; at label-free stops 1.4 and 3.2 worse than A); P-R held (depth 2 is 0.47 [0.06, 0.86] worse, [-0.14, 1.09] by patch). P-O failed: the shared-second-moment Adam was 0.83 [0.36, 1.33] worse than A, outside the margin of 1. P-path failed: the network formed the path's block on 11 of 36 windows (15 of 36 against the signed path; required 22); post hoc it did so on 11 of 14 windows whose signed path has no negative unit."

MUST NOT CLAIM: that the network "counts stars" or detects them better than detectors (no detector arm); that the mechanism is confirmed on real data (P-O and P-path failed); that Adam "forms hundreds of units" without "at 100000 steps" (flips sign at equal fit); that C or D "leave zero" or "stay dormant"; that the 114 is depth 1's intrinsic count error (it is at A's clock, budget-limited, with early or BIC stops 1.4); that the network "follows the path" on real data; that R1 (depth 2) is robust; "as accurate as the path" as a statement of accuracy (both are 0.5 to 0.75 above trivial baselines); that error 1.00 is independent of the flux-limit definition; that post hoc strata (11/14, dropping negatives, theta 0.85 criterion) rescue P-path; that selection leaked or was random over all windows (it conditions on 2 to 12 counted truth sources).
