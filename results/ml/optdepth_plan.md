## Question
The thesis of the paper: small-start training of a factorised Gaussian-unit model chooses its own unit count when (R) the readout has effective depth at least 3 and (O) the optimizer does not
normalise each coordinate by its own gradient history. The referee reports (`review/REVIEW_roundML.md` section 1, `review/REVIEW_roundT3BDF.md` sections D and F) separate what is proved from what
is numerical: the depth-p dormancy bound |w| <= (eps b)^(p/2) mu^(-p/(p-2)) (proved, exact unit), the formation margin ~ eps^((p-2)/2) (follows), sign descent and Adam with eps_adam = 0
erase the ordering by drive (proved for frozen drives), and one shared scalar second moment restores the formed set at lr >= s but not always the order (numerical, 3-unit counterexample for the order).
These two tests turn the theory into predictions that can fail, on targets and seeds no earlier file used. No claim is made beyond what the pre-registered rules below decide.

## P1. The optimizer
**Model.** The additive Gaussian-unit KAN [5, 1] of the spike, in numpy (torch is not installed): each edge is sum_k w_k exp(-(x - c_k)^2/(2 sigma_k^2)), M = 10 units per edge, initial centres on the grid of
[-2.5, 2.5], width the spacing, centres and log-widths trained, 1500 train / 500 validation noisy rows, 2000 clean test rows; factorised readout w = nu^2 A tanh(alpha/A), A = 3, alpha(0) = 0, nu(0)^2 = eps = 1e-3
(the numpy model `AddKan` of `theory3f_escape.py`, re-implemented in `exp_optdepth.py` and tested against it). All arms: the spike's `fit` (full batch, 0.5 * MSE, validation MSE every 25 steps, best iterate kept,
stop after 2000 steps without improvement counted from the first 10% drop, cap 12000 steps), activity rule tau = 0.02 (a unit is active when its rms contribution is at least 0.02 times the sd of the output).

**Arms** (same data, same start, same stopping rule):
1. `gd`: plain gradient descent with the frozen configuration of `results/ml/kan_freeze.json` (`ours_synth`: lr 1, group rates alpha, c, log sigma 1, nu 0.05, bias 0.1). Not tuned.
2. `adam`: Adam with the default eps_adam = 1e-8, one learning rate for all parameters; development grid lr in {0.003, 0.01, 0.03}.
3. `adam_big`: Adam with a large eps_adam and learning rate eps_adam times the group rates (GD-like rates: the update is m_hat * rates while the gradients are below eps_adam); grid eps_adam in {0.01, 0.1, 1}.
4. `shared_v`: Adam with ONE scalar second moment (the EMA of the mean over all coordinates of the squared gradient); grid lr in {0.003, 0.01, 0.03} x gradient pre-multiplied by the group rates (yes / no).
5. `sign`: sign descent (step lr * sign(gradient) per coordinate); grid lr in {0.0003, 0.001, 0.003, 0.01}.
6. `ctrl` (L1 + magnitude-prune control): the same units with free weights N(0, 0.1^2), Adam (lr 0.03, `kan_freeze.json` `lr_synth.ctrl`), L1 penalty lambda in {0, 3e-4, 1e-3, 3e-3, 1e-2, 3e-2},
   magnitude pruning at the activity rule, 600 steps of fine-tuning (Adam, lr 0.01); the penalty is chosen per run by validation (one-standard-error rule), as in the spike; the penalty chosen by the truth is
   reported as `ctrl_or` (oracle).
**Merge post-processing, for every method alike:** the active units of an edge are merged by single linkage on their centres (adjacent sorted centres closer than the radius are one cluster; the spike module
`exp_kan_merge.merged_count`). **Radius 0.3, declared here beforehand** (the true centres of one edge are at least 1.0 apart; the initial width is 0.556; the referee's natural range 0.3-0.55). The unmerged share is reported
next to it, and the other radii 0.1-0.5 as POST HOC.

**Tuning budget, development only.** Plain GD is frozen (one configuration, no tuning). Every other arm gets its grid above on the 12 development runs (3 targets x 2 noise x 2 seeds), chosen by the highest mean K>0
exact-edge share after merging at 0.3 (ties: the higher unmerged share, then grid order); that selection uses the truth, for every arm alike (the baselines are not disadvantaged: they get 3-6 candidates, GD none).
The control's lr and penalty grid are the frozen ones of the spike (not re-tuned). The chosen configurations are written to `optdepth_freeze.json` and committed before the test.

**Primary metric.** Exact-edge share on the edges with K > 0 (an edge is exact when its merged active-unit count equals the number K of true bumps). Per target: the mean over its 6 runs (2 noise x 3 seeds);
the statistic is the mean over the 20 targets; intervals are percentile intervals of a cluster bootstrap over the targets (20,000 resamples), paired for contrasts (differences are formed per target before resampling).
Secondary reported: all-edge share, K = 0 edges, surplus and missing units, runs exact on all five edges, clean-test RMSE, steps, runs at the cap.

**Predictions and decision rule (declared before any test run).** Theory (O): GD and the shared-second-moment optimizer keep the small-start ordering by drive and so choose the number of units; Adam and sign
descent normalise each coordinate by its own history and erase it.
* **(a)** GD and shared-v each exceed Adam and sign descent by at least 0.20 in the K>0 exact share at radius 0.3, each of the four paired contrasts with the lower end of its 95% cluster-bootstrap interval above 0.
  HELD only if all four hold.
* **(b)** GD and shared-v are within 0.10 of each other (point estimate of the paired difference, radius 0.3). HELD iff |GD - shared_v| <= 0.10.
* **(c)** No claim about the L1 + prune + merge control other than reporting.
* (d) Secondary, not part of the verdict: Adam with a large eps_adam is within 0.10 of GD (theory: it is then SGD).
The same rule is evaluated unmerged (radius 0) and at the other radii; those are labelled secondary and POST HOC respectively and do not change the verdict.
**Expectation recorded before the test (so that a failure is not rationalised afterwards):** the merge step also repairs the duplicated units of Adam and sign descent (the same mechanism the referee found for the control,
0.28 to 0.77), so (a) at radius 0.3 may well fail even though the unmerged contrast is large; and the referee's shared-v minus GD gap was +0.09, near the edge of (b). The rules are what they are.
Failed or capped runs stay in the data; nothing is dropped after scoring.

## P2. Depth
Closed-form unit (REVIEW_roundT3BDF D): gradient flow with rate ell on (u, v) from u = v = s, s^2 = eps b, drive g(t), eta = integral of g: depth 1: w = ell eta; depth 2: w = 2 eps b sinh(4 ell eta);
depth p >= 3: w = s^p[(1 - eta/lam)^-q - (1 + eta/lam)^-q], lam = s^-(p-2)/(ell p (p-2)), q = p/(p-2). If |eta| <= (1 - mu) lam then |w| <= s^p[mu^-q - (2 - mu)^-q] <= (eps b)^(p/2) mu^(-p/(p-2)).
Registered caveat from the referee: the hypotheses are on the actual dual eta of the network, not the path's; the network exponent below is an observed law whose margin is the realised one.

**P2.1 Scalar unit (control for the numerics).** One unit of the repository's readouts D1 ... D5 (`factorized_rbf`) driven by g(t) = d eta/dt, eta(t) = (1 - mu) lam sin(3 pi t) (the dual reaches margin mu of the wall
three times, both signs), mu in {0.25, 0.5}, eps in {1e-4, 1e-6, 1e-8, 1e-10}, DOP853 at rtol 1e-12. Rule: the log-log exponent of the largest |w| against eps is within **0.01 of p/2 for p = 3, 4, 5** (1.5, 2, 2.5),
within 0.02 of mu for p = 2 (the depth-2 law eps^mu) and within 0.01 of 0 for p = 1. HELD iff all hold.

**P2.2 The largest dormant coefficient on the repository's flow.** Flow of the depth-p readout (p = 1 ... 5) with moving centres (rate Gm = 12), horizon tau_end = 1.05 max(path entry times) + 1, two mass arms: **unequal
masses** b_i = exp(0.5 N(0, 1)) (fixed seed 4242; primary, as the task requires) and equal masses (secondary); eps in {1e-4, 1e-6, 1e-8, 1e-10}. Targets: the design and **8 targets** of the 200-sample with a finite path runner-up
margin (`margin_never`) of at least 0.15, sorted by that margin, at the positions round(linspace(0, n-1, 8)). Per target, depth and arm: the dormant population N* = units not formed (|w| <= min(0.01 max|A*|, 0.1 min|A*|)) at
the end at every valid eps; the largest dormant coefficient at an eps is the maximum over N* and over time of |w|; the exponent is the least-squares slope of log10 of it against log10 eps. A cell with a failed integration is dropped; a target
with fewer than 3 valid cells has no exponent for that depth; a target is excluded from the fit for a depth when the realised relative margin of the maximising unit (1 - |eta|/lam, with |eta|/lam inverted from the closed form of the unit; this
uses the identity w = W(eta), verified to 1e-7 by the referee) is below 0.10 at some eps. **Rule: the mean exponent over the included targets is within 0.05 of p/2 for each of p = 3, 4, 5** (HELD iff all three; unequal-masses arm primary).
Depth 2 (predicted exponent the margin mu of the path) and depth 1 (predicted 0) are contrasts, reported with no rule. As an identity check the report also prints the exponent after dividing by the closed-form bound at the realised margin (it is p/2 up to the
integration error if the unit is the closed form). Expectation recorded: p = 3 is the most likely to miss by more than 0.05, because the realised margin still drifts at eps = 1e-4 (the dual converges at rate eps^(1/2) for p = 3, referee D).

**P2.3 Formed-set agreement on the 200 benchmark targets.** For every target and p = 2, 3, 4, 5, eps in {1e-6, 1e-8}: the flow of the depth-p readout (equal masses, moving centres, the study's code path `exp_parametrization.cell`),
success = the formed set (|w| > min(0.01 max|A*|, 0.1 min|A*|) at the end) equals the final active set of the sliding inverse scale space path of the target; a failed integration is a failure. f_p(eps) = the success fraction over the 200 targets.
**Predicted ordering (stated beforehand), from the formation-margin law mu_theta(p, eps) = theta^(-(p-2)/p) (eps b)^((p-2)/2), b = 1, theta the formed threshold** (at eps = 1e-6: depth 3 ~ 4.6e-3, depth 4 ~ 1e-5, depth 5 ~ 2e-8 for theta = 0.01;
depth 2 logarithmic: ln(1/theta)/ln(1/eps) = 0.33 at 1e-6): a dormant unit of depth p is formed only if its margin is below mu_theta, which falls steeply with depth and with smaller eps. So: **f_2 < f_3 <= f_4 <= f_5 at each eps, and f_p(1e-8) >= f_p(1e-6) for every p**.
HELD iff all these point-estimate inequalities hold (f_2 < f_3 strictly). Reported with paired cluster-bootstrap intervals over the targets, with the order-of-entry agreement and the number of failed integrations; if f_3 = f_4 = f_5 = 1 at both eps the ordering is a tie and the report says
it is saturated. Secondary (no rule): the per-target prediction "success iff margin_never(path) > mu_theta(p, eps)", its accuracy and confusion table.

## Size and compute
P1: 120 runs per arm; per run the plain arms take 7-13 s each and the control about 36 s (numpy, one core); about 90 s per run, about 3 hours for the test. P2: scalar 36 integrations; network 9 targets x 2 arms x 5 depths x 4 eps = 360 flows (about 1-2 s each);
benchmark 200 targets x 4 depths x 2 eps = 1600 flows (about 1 s each, depth 2 faster). One core, `nice`.

## What will be dropped
Nothing is dropped after scoring. A prediction that fails is reported as failed with its numbers. Anything computed after the test results are seen is labelled POST HOC (the radius sweep and any extra diagnostic).
