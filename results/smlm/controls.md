# Controls for the SMLM comparison: the order of entry, the symmetry of the Picasso tuning, the development-versus-test gap

Generated by `experiments/exp_smlm_controls.py` from `controls_raw.npz`; every number below is printed by the script. **Post hoc**: the analyses A1-A3 were declared in the script's docstring after the results of `iss.md`, `smlm2013.md` and `picasso_baseline.md` were known, in answer to three objections (the order of entry may be irrelevant; the ISS and Picasso are tuned on different objectives; no development-versus-test gap is reported). Nothing in the existing reports, raws or scripts is changed; they were read (digests of the files the references were copied from: iss_raw.npz ddb159c4b34d, picasso_ladder_raw.npz 04ab6c77f1ce, smlm2013_raw.npz 3b05d2abf4be). Frame indices are 0-based stack indices, positions in nm; metrics pooled over the test frames with 95 % intervals of a bootstrap over blocks of 10 consecutive frames (1000 resamples, seed 0); differences are paired (the same resampled blocks for both methods). A paired difference is a **win** (for the first method) when its interval excludes 0 in its favour, a **loss** when it excludes 0 against it, a **tie** otherwise. Python 3.13.18, numpy 2.5.4.

## What this does not establish

* **Same operating point is not the best greedy.** A1a keeps the dual's development-chosen kappa, width bound and offset, which were tuned for the dual path; A1b gives the greedy path its own tuning where it was run.
* **Frames only.** Intervals cover the sampling of frames (blocks of 10), not other datasets or the simulation; the development and test frames share the structures. The 2016 sets share one structure, the 2013 sets two, and within each pair the sets are not independent samples.
* **The Picasso re-selection is a local search** (a coordinate ascent over the original grids from the original start), not the global optimum of the offset-corrected objective, as the original was not for the raw one. It reproduces the stored search space, not its path: the stored R2E / R2J configurations are listed next to the new ones and their offset-corrected development values are given.
* **The offset is estimated per configuration on the development frames** (mean signed error of the matched pairs), as in both existing pipelines; it is a constant, so it cannot change which emitters are detected except through the re-pairing at the 250 nm tolerance.
* **Post hoc.** The analyses were chosen after the existing results were known; they were declared in the docstring before they were run, and the existing reports are unchanged.
