Generated by `experiments/exp_smlm2013.py` from `smlm2013_raw.npz`; every number below is printed by the script. Frame indices are 0-based stack indices, positions in nm (corner convention), metrics pooled over the frames with 95 % block-bootstrap intervals (blocks of 10 consecutive frames, 1000 resamples, seed 0); differences are paired (the same resampled blocks for both methods). Python 3.13.18, numpy 2.5.4, Picasso 0.11.3.

**What these sets are.** The four training sets of the 2013 challenge with public ground truth (the hub's page: "generated with a 2D theoretical PSF"): a realistic tubulin structure at low (Tubulins_I) and high (Tubulins_II) read-out noise, 256 x 256 px of 150 nm, and bundles of 8 tubes at high (Bundled HD) and low (Bundled LS) emitter density, 64 x 64 px of 100 nm. They differ from the four 2016 sets of `iss.md` in the structure, the pixel size, the camera (QE 1, 1 DN per electron), the PSF (a Gaussian of about 100 nm without halo, against 140 nm with a long halo) and the background (structured, time-varying autofluorescence), and in the ground truth (one-frame activations). The pages state "168 frames" for the high-density bundles and "2400 frames" for the tubulins; the files hold 361 and 2401 (`smlm2013.py`). The ground truth is `fluorophores/activation.csv` of each `fluorophores.zip` (the `oracle.zip` files hold only projection images); it was checked equal to the per-frame files. The ISS recipe (tiles, crowded fit) and the Picasso grids are adapted to the sets as declared in the script's docstring; **the long sequences are used in part** (frame ranges below), the dense set in 30 development and 60 test frames.

## What this experiment does not establish

* **The PSF family.** The simulated PSF is a Gaussian, which the ISS kernels (pixel-integrated Gaussians of sliding width) contain exactly, while Picasso fits a point-sampled Gaussian: on the 2016 sets, whose PSF has a halo that Picasso's Gaussian does not contain, the ISS's kernels (a core plus a halo part, worth a point or two of efficiency on the development frames) match the PSF better than Picasso's, so the match is not specific to the 2013 sets; whether it decides the 2013 wins was not tested in this experiment. A real PSF is neither. The measured width and photon scale of the images also differ from the shipped PSF file (see each set's calibration paragraph); the cause was not investigated.
* **Four sets, two structures, two noise levels.** The two tubulin sets share the ground truth and differ in the noise only; the two bundle sets share the 81,049 fluorophore positions and differ in the density. They are not four independent samples, and the dense set (224 emitters per 64 x 64 frame) needed its own recipe (declared in the docstring), so its row is a statement about that recipe.
* **Frame ranges.** The long sequences are used in part (declared ranges); the dense set has 60 test frames (6 blocks of the block bootstrap). The intervals cover the sampling of frames only.
* **The efficiency of the dense set** is dominated by its RMSE (pairs within 250 nm of an overlapped neighbour), not by detection; a method that matches more emitters is penalised on RMSE.
* **The challenge setting** (450 nm border, dimmest quarter dropped after pairing) is the harness's reconstruction of the 2016 rules; whether the 2013 assessment used the same is not known.
* **The noise law's slope** (1.41, the page's EM factor) was not calibrated on the data (the tubulin sets give 1.5-1.6 by a free fit); the sensitivity of the result to it was not tested. Only the constant b0 is measured. The background is a static surface from the development frames (its stability is reported per set).
* **Tuning effort.** The ISS searches 84 operating points per set (no second round: entry width and resolution-limit variants were not run), Picasso 12762 to 19063 scored configurations; both on the same development frames. The ISS recipe changes (tiles, scaled lengths, the dense recipe) were forced by the data and declared before the test runs, but they are choices.
* **Time.** The ISS is two to three orders of magnitude slower per frame than Picasso (table above); the timings come from a shared machine and mix wall-clock and CPU time as stated.

### Calibration and the ISS recipe

Calibration (development frames 0-39 of Tubulins_I; no labels except for sigma0): background surface mean 72.65 photons (range -2.2 to 279.4); noise law var = a x + b0 with a = 1.41 (the page's EM factor) and **b0 = 210.6 photon^2** (the page's read-out sd 10 gives 100); robust high-frequency variance against the smooth level (level: variance, model; the bins above the 90th percentile of the level hold the emitters of a sparse frame and are not used for b0): -1: 197, 211; 26: 245, 248; 66: 305, 304; 117: 383, 376; 229: 547, 533; the free slope through all bins is 1.52 (span of the levels 230 photons). **sigma0 = 0.682 px (102 nm)**: median over 463 isolated bright emitters of the development frames of Tubulins_I of the width of a pixel-integrated Gaussian plus a plane (IQR 0.581 to 0.743; 5 to 95 % 0.362 to 0.828); the page's description (FWHM 258.21 nm at the focal plane, growing exponentially to twice that at 500 nm) gives 0.896 px at the median |z| of the sample (147 nm) and 0.731 px in focus; the fitted photons are 0.76 (median; IQR 0.55 to 0.90) of the ground-truth photons; the fit minus truth offset is (-0.1, 0.1) nm (median). The widths slide in [0.487, 1.558] px, r_ex = 0.974 px, width bounds of the post-filter 0.974, 1.217, 1.558 px.

The PSF file shipped with the set (`psf.zip`, a stack of planes of 10 nm voxels, each summing to 1; the page's PSF description is its z = 0 plane, FWHM 258.21 nm): second-moment sigma and the width that the same pixel-integrated Gaussian fit finds in a noise-free camera rendering of it at 150 nm pixels (nm), by plane: z 0 nm: 110 / 110; z 50 nm: 117 / 117; z 100 nm: 126 / 126; z 200 nm: 145 / 145; z 290 nm: 164 / 164. The widths measured on the images above (sigma0 = 102 nm, median over the emitters at |z| = 147 nm) are smaller than any rendering of this PSF at the emitters' depths (117 nm at z = 50 nm), the fitted photons are below the ground-truth photons while the PSF planes are normalized: the images do not follow the shipped PSF exactly (the cause was not investigated); the experiment uses the measured width.

Stability of the background (label-free, measured after the test run, not used anywhere): the smoothed temporal median of the test frames minus that of the calibration frames has rms 15.34 photons (|max| 64.3; mean 8.61), against the pixel noise sd of 17.7 photons at the mean background.

Recipe: tiles of 64 x 64 px cores with 8 px margins, the path stopped at the first entrant below kappa = 4; entries per frame 84.3 on average (test, default configuration), tiles at the cap of 40 entries in the test run: 0.

### Calibration and the ISS recipe

Calibration (development frames 0-299 of Bundled_Tubes_Long_Sequence; the dense set uses the calibration of its sparse set; no labels except for sigma0): background surface mean 39.13 photons (range 30.6 to 42.5); noise law var = a x + b0 with a = 1.41 (the page's EM factor) and **b0 = 126.9 photon^2** (the page's read-out sd 5 gives 25); robust high-frequency variance against the smooth level (level: variance, model; the bins above the 90th percentile of the level hold the emitters of a sparse frame and are not used for b0): 33: 173, 173; 38: 181, 181; 40: 187, 184; 43: 192, 187; 61: 1222, 214; the slope is not determinable from the bins here (the levels span only 29 photons). **sigma0 = 0.997 px (100 nm)**: median over 553 isolated bright emitters of the development frames of Bundled_Tubes_Long_Sequence of the width of a pixel-integrated Gaussian plus a plane (IQR 0.967 to 1.030; 5 to 95 % 0.934 to 1.073); the page's description (FWHM 258.21 nm at the focal plane, growing exponentially to twice that at 300 nm) gives 1.226 px at the median |z| of the sample (48 nm) and 1.097 px in focus; the fitted photons are 0.90 (median; IQR 0.85 to 0.94) of the ground-truth photons; the fit minus truth offset is (0.3, 0.5) nm (median). The widths slide in [0.712, 2.279] px, r_ex = 1.425 px, width bounds of the post-filter 1.425, 1.781, 2.279 px.

The PSF file shipped with the set (`psf.zip`, a stack of planes of 10 nm voxels, each summing to 1; the page's PSF description is its z = 0 plane, FWHM 258.21 nm): second-moment sigma and the width that the same pixel-integrated Gaussian fit finds in a noise-free camera rendering of it at 100 nm pixels (nm), by plane: z 0 nm: 110 / 110; z 50 nm: 123 / 123; z 100 nm: 138 / 138; z 200 nm: 174 / 174; z 290 nm: 214 / 214. The widths measured on the images above (sigma0 = 100 nm, median over the emitters at |z| = 48 nm) are smaller than any rendering of this PSF at the emitters' depths (123 nm at z = 50 nm), the fitted photons are below the ground-truth photons while the PSF planes are normalized: the images do not follow the shipped PSF exactly (the cause was not investigated); the experiment uses the measured width.

Recipe (crowded): refits of the kernels within 4 px of an entrant only, window 10 px beyond, no final polish, at most 400 entries and no early stop, the final block cleaned by the units' own significance (rule "clean"); entries per frame 400 on average (the cap binds in every frame by design: the path runs on into the noise).
