# SMLM spot check of the enter/exit limit cycle of `imaging.sliding_path`

Generated by `experiments/exp_cycle_scan.py` from `cycle_scan_raw.npz`; every number below is printed by the script. The defect (`results/astro/cyclefix_plan.md`): a lattice kernel enters on the correlation at its lattice start, the block fit returns its coefficient 0, the unit leaves on the correlation at its slid centre, and it re-enters at dt = 0, at the same entry time, until the entry cap. The guard (`src/core/imaging_guard.py`) bars a lattice point that has exited twice at the same entry time. Here the frames are fitted by the experiments' own functions (`exp_smlm_iss.run_frames`, `exp_smlm2013.run_frames`) at the tuned (E-optimal) operating point of the experiment and at its a priori default, with the guarded path function in place of `imaging.sliding_path`; **an activation** is a lattice point barred in a frame (a tile for the tiled 2013 sets). For every frame with an activation and for the first 3 frames without one (a control: a path without a double exit at one entry time is the unguarded path bit for bit) the frame is fitted again with the frozen, unguarded function and the detections are compared. Detections: x, y (nm), photons, width (px) at the operating point's rule; "changed" means not equal bit for bit. The E and J tables are not recomputed.

## Limits

* Only the first 40 test frames of each set were scanned (the experiments' test ranges are 100 to 900 frames), and only the first 3 frames without an activation were compared with an unguarded run; for the others the guard's property (no double exit at one entry time: the path is the unguarded one) is relied on, and the controls test it.
* The guard acts only on repeated entries at one entry time; a frame's cycle that advances tau is not seen here.
* For the tiled 2013 sets an activation is counted per tile and the entries per frame are the sums over the tiles.
