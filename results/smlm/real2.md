## Not done, deviations, weaknesses

* See plan sections 1, 4 and 5 for the weaknesses declared in advance: T1 is the same field (and pores) as the first D1 test, with new frames; D2 has three recordings of one preparation and no between-microscope variance; the reference is derived from third-party tables of other frames of the same recordings; Part B is a manufactured density with the table as reference.
* Grid edge: the development optimum of the ISS4 ascent is at the TOP of the extended kappa grid (50) for 0 of 8 configurations of D1 and 6 of 8 of D2 (the plan did not extend the grid further; the frozen point is at kappa 15 (D1) and 5 (D2)).
* The stored raw keeps every frozen choice, the per-configuration development results and the test paths; the development ISS paths (49 MB, `devpath|*`) were removed from it after the freeze to keep the file small (`--step dev` recomputes them; the freeze hash does not cover them).
* Computed locally on a shared 4-core machine (no Kaggle); the optional offer to move the long jobs to private CPU notebooks was not taken because the setup would have cost more than the jobs.
* No raw-frame DNA-origami or calibration-bead data with a known spacing were reachable (plan section 1).
* The greedy-order row was not run in this study (the controls of `results/smlm/controls.md` and `results/astro/controls.md` cover the order of entry).
* Everything labelled POST-AMENDMENT (the verdict for T1 under Amendment 1) is not part of the registered verdict.
