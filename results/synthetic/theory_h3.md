## C. The certificate against the repository's path

MIXED TEST, to be read as such. The certificate computes the entries from the duals with the blocks at their critical points near the features and checks the dual conditions (a)-(b) -- all in the repository's inner product (61 samples of [-1, 1], the 21 starting kernels, equal walls) -- and the box (c) of Proposition A, which is a CONTINUUM condition (the proposition assumes <phi_c phi_c'> = Z Gamma(c - c')). "continuum-certified" = (c) holds in the continuum and (a)-(b) hold in the samples. The sampled Gram departs from the continuum near the window's edge (Part W): "interior" = every feature at least 3.5 widths from the edge (|c| <= 0.58), where the departures are <= 1e-4 (Part W) and are taken as absorbed by requiring (c) with the budget Omega = 0.01 a_min (a perturbation of the Gram entries by delta moves the gradient and the Hessian by O(delta (R + v_bar)), dominated by Omega when delta <= 1e-3; this absorption is argued, not proved here); "interior-certified" = interior, (a)-(b), and (c) with that budget: only these are covered by the theorem for the sampled path.
