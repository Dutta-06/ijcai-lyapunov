### 1.2b How much the covariate diagnosis can bear (POST HOC)

The roundness of the PSF stamp is a hypothesis at n = 5 clusters with 16 covariates, not a finding. The covariates are constant within a frame and the patches of a frame share them, so the 19 patches are pseudo-replicates of 5 clusters; the cluster-level numbers below use the 5 cluster means (gap and covariate), leave-one-cluster-out as before, and a permutation test of the cluster gaps: all 120 assignments of the 5 gaps to the 5 clusters, and the share of them in which the BEST of the 16 covariates predicts at least as well as the observed best (the null that none of the covariates relates to the gap, with the search over 16 covariates included).

* ISS iso minus crowdsource, cluster means: stamp ellipticity LOCO rmse 0.105 against the null 0.130, signs 4 / 5, Spearman -0.70 (n = 5); the best of the 16 covariates is rsd_iso (rmse 0.050 against 0.130, signs 4 / 5). Permutation p-value that some covariate does as well as that best: 14 / 120 = 0.12; for the stamp ellipticity alone 8 / 120 = 0.07.
* ISS rule minus crowdsource, cluster means: stamp ellipticity LOCO rmse 0.083 against the null 0.069, signs 3 / 5, Spearman -0.40 (n = 5); the best of the 16 covariates is comp2122_iso (rmse 0.065 against 0.069, signs 3 / 5). Permutation p-value that some covariate does as well as that best: 82 / 120 = 0.68; for the stamp ellipticity alone 74 / 120 = 0.62.

So: the stamp roundness is a candidate explanation, one hypothesis among several, from 5 clusters; the best covariate for the isotropic gap is itself an output of the same ISS fit (the residual scale), and the sky, noise and density covariates also beat the null (the erratum above). The report does not claim that roundness predicts or explains the dependence.

## 2b. The first attempt at a repair, examined on seven spent cases (POST HOC, development): a noise model with a model-error floor

Weights 1 / sqrt(s0^2 var0 + eta^2 qd^2): var0 the frozen variance, qd the smoothed absolute value of the high-passed model of the first-pass catalogue, (s0 >= 1, eta >= 0) fitted to that catalogue's own residual (`astro_clusterdep.calibrate`; label-free); then the whole guarded path is run again with these weights (`run_path_weighted`: the frozen program with other weights), read out at kappa 2 (the tuned value), 3 and 5. It is the principled form of "the residual scale of the round-kernel catalogue predicts the gap".
