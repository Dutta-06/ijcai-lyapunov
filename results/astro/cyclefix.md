## 1. The defect and the guard

`imaging.sliding_path` lets a lattice kernel enter when its dual reaches the wall; the entry test uses the correlation at the kernel's *lattice start* (`v`). A unit leaves when its coefficient is 0 and the correlation at its *slid centre* (`g`) is not positive; its dual then goes back to the wall (`U = rho`), so a kernel with `v > 0` re-enters at `dt = 0`, at the same entry time. If the block fit of the crowded cluster returns its coefficient 0 each time, the path loops at one tau until `max_entries` (2500); the time rule (`tau_max`) never acts because tau does not advance. Such a run stops after about 150 distinct entries whatever its stopping rule says, and the cap flag of the raw files (`bic = [block, hist length, 1]`) is the signature of the cycle, not of a long path (found by referee round REP, finding 2; repeat patch m2r3 (110, 501), a priori default: the lattice point (110, 54) enters and exits 2345 times at tau = 2.18969).

The guard (`src/core/imaging_guard.sliding_path_guarded`): a lattice point that has exited twice at the same entry time (the two times within 1e-12 x max(1, tau): the repeats of a cycle are at exactly the same tau, since the re-entry is at dt = 0) is barred from entering for the rest of the path; the result lists the barred points (`barred`). A first exit followed by a successful re-entry is untouched, so a path without a double exit at one entry time is the unguarded path bit for bit, and `cycle_guard=False` is `imaging.sliding_path` bit for bit (tests: `tests/test_imaging_guard.py`). The guard removes the repeated entry, not its cause (the entry test at the lattice start and the exit test at the slid centre disagree). The re-runs below call the frozen run functions (`exp_astro_m2.run_path`, `exp_astro_psf.run_path_psf`) with `imaging.sliding_path` replaced, in the worker process, by a wrapper of the guarded function that keeps the barred points and the event counts in a side channel.

## 7. Limitations

* **The corrected rows are post hoc.** The defect was found by a referee after the results had been seen; the guard was written after that; every corrected row says so and none is held out.
* **The guard changes the path, it does not restore "what the unguarded algorithm would have done".** After a lattice point is barred, its neighbours enter in its place; the corrected rows are the rows of the guarded algorithm. The guard removes repeated entries at one entry time; a cycle whose turns advance tau, or a point that exits and re-enters at different times, is not removed. The largest number of entries of one lattice point and the number of points entered three times or more are printed for every guarded re-run (table in section 3); a point entered 5 times or more is flagged.
* **The guard keys on the entry time, so it never acts in `order='greedy'`** (tau is the step count there); the design variant `greedy_snr` is the one greedy astronomy path.
* **A cap reached again is not "the" path.** Where a guarded re-run reaches 2500 entries again its rows are cut by the cap; the report says whether it did so with or without activations (section 3), and the development table flags such rows in its capped column.
* **Check mode: 65 of 65 paths were run.** All of them were run.
* **Identity means identity in this environment** (same Python, numpy, scipy and machine type as the stored runs; the stored runs were reproduced bit for bit wherever the guard did not act). The run times are those of a shared, loaded machine and are not comparable with the stored ones.
* **Added by the scan, not by the brief:** the design variant `norex` stored 2500 entries but no `bic` record; it is re-run in stage 1 and labelled as an addition.
* **Derived tables.** `psf.md` compares the adequate kernel with the *frozen* M92 default rows and prints their residual scale; those rows are the capped ones (section 5.4 gives the corrected residual scale and section 5.5 reprints the M92 part of `psf.md` with the corrected rows; the other tables of `psf.md` are not regenerated here). `m2.md` and `repeat.md` are not regenerated; the coordinator decides how the first outputs are kept.
* **Intervals** are conditional on the patches (blocks of 20 x 20 px resampled inside fixed patches), as in the frozen reports; windows and truth are shared between the frames.
