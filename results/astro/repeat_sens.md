# Post hoc sensitivity analyses of the held-out repeat-frame test (finished test: nothing here changes it)

Generated by `experiments/exp_astro_repeat_sens.py` from `repeat_sens_raw.npz`; every number below is printed by the script. **POST HOC.** The held-out repeat-frame test (`repeat.md`, `repeat_plan.md`, `repeat_raw.npz`, `freeze_repeat.json`) was finished and its result seen before any analysis below was made; the controls are those of the adversarial referee of round REP (`review/REVIEW_roundREP.md`, findings 1, 3, 4, 5, 6, 9, 10), ported here as committed, tested code. None of it changes `repeat.md`, the plan or a freeze, and nothing in the project's methods or configuration may change on its strength. The analyses re-count the STORED detections with the truth moved, other tolerances, other depth cuts, other magnitudes or other block sizes; no path is re-run. The exact definitions were written into the script's docstring before the first analysis was run (the one change after the first star counts is listed in section 11). Scoring: `astro_common.score` at its default (0.5 px, 0.5 mag, one to one, truth within 2.5 px of the edge not scored, F of completeness and purity at mag < 22), re-implemented with the tolerances as arguments (`rescore`); at the default it reproduces all 60 stored rows of `repeat_raw.npz` (counts, 25-block arrays, median offsets) exactly. Intervals: 95 % percentile intervals of a block bootstrap over the 20 x 20 px blocks (2000 resamples, seed 0, stratified within each patch, the same resamples for every row of a comparison, so differences are paired), conditional on the patches; other settings are labelled. Verdicts (win / tie / loss: the paired interval is above, around, below 0) are descriptive: no hypothesis was declared for them and no multiplicity correction is made. A row marked "defective path: yes" (or D) contains an a priori default run of the isotropic kernel that hit the entry cap, an enter/exit limit cycle of `imaging.sliding_path` (section 8): it is never pooled silently.

## 11. Limits, deviations, what was not done

* **Post hoc.** The same patches, the same detections and the same truth as the finished test; the analyses were chosen after the result was seen, by a referee. They cannot confirm or revise the declared outcomes P1 to P3; they say how far those verdicts depend on a convention (the alignment of the truth, the tolerances, the depth, the block size, the zero point, the defective runs).
* **One frame, two patches for P1; four sky windows, three exposures for P2.** The intervals are conditional on the patches. The truth (HUGS) and its defects (HST-saturated stars, stars without an F606W) are shared by all frames and with M2.
* **The BIC readout is not re-scored.** The detections of the BIC readouts are not stored; their rows are the stored counts (alpha 0, 0.5 px / 0.5 mag, m < 22, 20 px blocks). The tuned and the a priori default readouts are re-scored from the stored detections at kappa 2 and 5.
* **The offsets are estimates.** The scan offsets are the rigid shifts of the PSF-convolved truth model that minimise chi^2 (per patch); the own offsets are medians of 9 to 15 star fits per frame (selection B), with the neighbour contamination of section 1; neither separates the SDSS astrometry from the proper motion over the epoch difference, and neither is a measurement of the kernels' own positional bias.
* **Defective runs.** 3 a priori default runs of the isotropic kernel are defective paths; this script marks them and gives the pooled rows with and without them, and does not replace them. The guarded re-runs are another script's work (`exp_astro_cyclefix.py`). The optional hook `--run --cyclefix PATH` copies their rows from its raw (the key format is in the script's docstring); it was not used for this raw (no section 8.1).
* **Deviation from the declared design.** Selection A of the star fits, as declared, left 3, 2, 3 stars on m2r1, m2r2, m2r3 (kept after the fit; 3, 2, 3 selected); the frames below the declared minimum of 3 stars are: m2r2. Selection B (the geometry of the referee's `template_fit.py`) was written into the docstring as an amendment after those NUMBERS were seen (and before any selection-B offset was computed), and the `own` scenarios use it; selection A is reported beside it. The amendment also states that a patch with fewer than 3 selected stars uses the frame median in the scenario `ownp`.
* **Not done.** No path was re-run (the BIC catalogues at a shifted truth and the guarded default runs need re-runs); the post hoc M53 and M92 kernel gains were not tested for the same offset sensitivity (only their offsets are measured, section 1); proper motion, `fpM` masks and cosmic rays were not examined; the cause of the position difference of the two kernels (section 10) is open.
