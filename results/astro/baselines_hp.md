Question (referee): does a baseline given the ISS's front end (the border-consistent high-pass of the frame and of the PSF stamp, the ISS's weights) and a search of its own pipeline close the gap to the sliding ISS? Scoring and intervals as `exp_astro_m2`: match at 0.5 px and 0.5 mag, F-measure of completeness and purity at mag < 22, 95 % percentile intervals of a bootstrap over the 20 x 20 px blocks (2000 resamples, seed 0, the same resampled blocks for every row of a table, so differences are paired; the pooled tables resample the blocks within each patch). The tuned ISS rows and the old baselines are READ from the frozen results and influenced nothing declared or selected here; nothing is re-tuned on a test patch. Deviations of the plan from the brief (plan, section 10): the threshold of photutils is in the variance model's pixel noise, not the MAD of the crowded high-passed image; the iteration counts are {3, 10, 30} (mode new) and 6 (mode all); the crowdsource family searches the detection threshold and the front end, with no post-filters. **On the repeat frames these baselines are added later** (`repeat_plan.md`, section 2: chosen from a declared family on the two M2 development patches only, their catalogues written to a file of their own, `baseline_catalogs_hp.npz`, before they were scored); they belong to none of the declared outcomes P1 to P3 of that plan, which concern the ISS.
