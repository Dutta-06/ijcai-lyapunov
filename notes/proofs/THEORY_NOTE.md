## 0. What is claimed and what is not (read this first; revised after the referee round)

**Proved here (complete proofs below):**
1. **Lemma 1.1.** The inner problem of H3, the fast block flow and the *network's own* $\varepsilon$-flow are Riemannian gradient flows of the fit $F$ with a positive diagonal mobility.
2. **Proposition A (coupled entry layer, continuum, explicit constants).** For $K$ Gaussian features of the kernels' width at separation $\ge\Delta$, amplitude ratio $R$, an initial point in a box of radius $r>d_0$ ($d_0$ the entrant's starting offset): if the explicit inequalities (L),(C),(N) hold, the layer stays in the box, converges to the unique nondegenerate critical point of the next block, and the entrant's coefficient crosses every level $<v_{\rm lo}a_{\min}$ once and transversally. The conditions involve neither the mobility, the mass, the walls nor the length of the slide (a property of Prop. A only), so the sketch's "exponentially small $\delta_0$" does not arise. Thresholds $\Delta_{\rm entry}$ (ball arithmetic, grid step 0.02) at $d_0=h/(2\sigma)=0.4167$: $K=2$: 4.66 ($R=1$), 5.50 (2.4), 6.70 (10); $K=3$: 5.08 (1), 5.84 (2.4). Equivalently $\delta_0=\Gamma(\Delta-2r)\lesssim0.0265$ ($K=2$, $R=1$). $\Delta_{\rm entry}$ is a *conservative sufficient condition*, not a transition.
3. **Theorem K.** For the limit object (Def. 1) with **equal walls**, if the finite, generic checks (a),(b) hold and $\Delta\ge\max_k\Delta_{\rm entry}(K,R,d_0^k)$ (with $d_0^k$ the offset of the $k$-th entrant, which need not be the nearest kernel: a per-target check), the path is the recursion (piecewise-linear duals + blocks at the critical points of Prop. A): exactly $K$ entries, no exit, final block = target, (H1)-(H3), and (H4) only at levels $<v_{\rm lo}a_{\min}$. The separation requirement is Prop. A's; it does not involve $\mu_0,\varpi$.

## 1. Setting and the reduction of the inner problem to a gradient flow with a product structure

Notation. Lengths are in units of the kernel width $\sigma$ (so $\Gamma(d)=e^{-d^2/4}$, $\Gamma'(d)=-\tfrac d2\Gamma(d)$). Continuum inner product,
$\langle\varphi(\cdot;c)\varphi(\cdot;c')\rangle=Z\,\Gamma(c-c')$; dividing $F$ by $Z$ only rescales the inner time, so $Z=1$ below.
Target $f^*=\sum_{k\le K}A_k\varphi(\cdot;c_k^*)$, $a_k=|A_k|$, $|c^*_k-c^*_l|\ge\Delta$ ($k\ne l$), $R=\max a_k/\min a_k$, and we normalise $\min_k a_k=1$ (see Remark 1.2).
A unit assigned to feature $k(i)$ has the coefficient $w_i=\epsilon_iv_i$, $\epsilon_i=\operatorname{sgn}A_{k(i)}$, $v_i>0$, and the centre $c_i=c^*_{k(i)}+d_i$. Its \emph{error function}
is $e_i=a_{k(i)}\varphi(\cdot;c^*_{k(i)})-v_i\varphi(\cdot;c_i)$ (so $A_{k(i)}\varphi^*_{k(i)}-w_i\varphi_i=\epsilon_ie_i$). With $f_U$ the unfitted features,
$$F=\tfrac12\Big\langle\Big(\sum_i\epsilon_ie_i+f_U\Big)^2\Big\rangle=\sum_i S_i+\sum_{i<j}\epsilon_i\epsilon_j\langle e_i,e_j\rangle+\sum_i\epsilon_i\langle e_i,f_U\rangle+\tfrac12\langle f_U^2\rangle,\qquad S_i=\tfrac12\big(a_i^2-2v_ia_i\Gamma(d_i)+v_i^2\big).\tag{1.1}$$

**Lemma 1.1 (the inner problem is a gradient flow with a positive diagonal mobility).** Every flow met in the supplement for the units of a block and an entrant --- the inner
problem of Hypothesis H3 (Lemma S2(i)), the fast flow \eqref{eq:flow} on the critical curve, and the $\varepsilon$-flow of the network itself (Lemma S1(o),(ii)) --- has the form
$$\dot v_i=-M_i(v_i)\,\partial_{v_i}F,\qquad \dot c_i=-\mu_c\,\partial_{c_i}F,\tag{1.2}$$
with $M_i:(0,\infty)\to(0,\infty)$ continuous and locally Lipschitz and $\mu_c>0$ a constant ($\mu_c=Gm$). *Proof.* $\partial w/\partial\eta=W'(\eta)>0$ and $\dot\eta=-\partial_wF$ give
$\dot w=-W'(W^{-1}(w))\partial_wF$; on the critical curve $W=\mathcal W_\rho$, $M(v)=|\mathcal W'(\mathcal W^{-1}(v))|/\rho$, which behaves like $c\,v^{4/3}$ at $0^+$ and like $\kappa v$ at $\infty$; the centres obey
$\dot c=Gm\,w\langle r\nabla_c\varphi\rangle=-Gm\,\partial_cF$. \qed

The only properties of $M_i,\mu_c$ used below are positivity and local Lipschitz continuity. **Nothing below depends on the mobility, the mass, the walls or the length of the slide.**

*Remark 1.2 (normalisation).* The sufficient conditions below are invariant under $F\to F/a_{\min}^2$, $v\to v/a_{\min}$, $d\to d$ [the Hessian in $(v/a_{\min},d)$ has entries $1$, $a(d/2)\Gamma/a_{\min}$, $va(\tfrac12-\tfrac{d^2}4)\Gamma/a_{\min}^2$], so $a_{\min}=1$ is no restriction; $R$ is the only amplitude parameter.

## 2. Proposition A (the coupled entry layer, with explicit constants)

The proposition is for the **continuum** Gram $\langle\varphi(\cdot;c)\varphi(\cdot;c')\rangle=Z\Gamma(c-c')$ (translation invariant); for the repository's 61-sample window see Part W of the report and Section 4C. Fix a **box** $B=\{|d_i|\le r\ \forall i;\ v_i\in[v_{\rm lo},\bar v]\}$ for the units $i\in S\cup\{e\}$ ($S$ the block, $e$ the entrant), $0<r<\sqrt2$; the entrant's lower face is its starting value $v_e(0)=v_0\in(0,v_{\rm lo}]$.
Put $\ell=\Delta-2r$, $g=\Gamma(\ell)$, $\psi(x)=\tfrac x2\Gamma(x)$, $|\Gamma''(x)|=(\tfrac{x^2}4-\tfrac12)\Gamma(x)$ (decreasing for $x\ge\sqrt6$), $\kappa=(K-1)g$, and for $\Omega\ge0$ (the total coefficient, in units of $a_{\min}$, of units outside the box; $\Omega=0$ in the limit object)
$$\bar v=\frac{R(1+\kappa)+\Omega}{1-\kappa}\,(1+0^+),\quad E_v=\kappa(R+\bar v)+\Omega,\quad E_c=(K-1)(R+\bar v)\psi(\ell)+0.43\,\Omega,\quad v_{\rm lo}=\Gamma(r)-E_v,\quad \tilde d=\frac{2E_c}{\Gamma(r)}.$$
$\;$(the constant $0.43>\sup|\Gamma'|=e^{-1/2}/\sqrt2$). Let $\lambda_{\rm own}=\tfrac12\big(1+t-\sqrt{(1-t)^2+4s^2}\big)$ with $s=R\psi(\tilde d)$, $t=(\Gamma(\tilde d)-E_v)(\tfrac12-\tfrac{\tilde d^2}4)\Gamma(\tilde d)$, and
$$H_{\rm c}=\max\{(K-1)[g+(R+\bar v)\psi(\ell)+\bar v\psi(\ell)]+0.43\Omega,\ (K-1)[(R+\bar v)\psi(\ell)+\bar v(R+\bar v)|\Gamma''|(\ell)+\bar v\psi(\ell)+\bar v^2|\Gamma''|(\ell)]+0.43\Omega+\tfrac12\bar v\Omega\}.$$

> **Proposition A.** Let $\ell\ge\sqrt6$, $\kappa<1$ and
> **(L)** $v_{\rm lo}>0$ (i.e. $\Gamma(r)>E_v$); **(C)** $\psi(r)>E_c$; **(N)** $\tilde d<r$, $\Gamma(\tilde d)>E_v$ and $\lambda_{\rm own}>H_{\rm c}$.
> Let the initial point lie in $B$: the block at the critical point of the previous layer (interior to $B$) and the entrant at $|d_e(0)|\le d_0<r$ (**strictly**), $v_e(0)=v_0\in(0,v_{\rm lo}]$. (The proof of (i) uses only that the initial point is in $B$; that the block is at a critical point of its own fit is not a hypothesis, only the case of use.) Then for every mobility as in Lemma 1.1
> (i) the trajectory of (1.2) stays in $B$ for all $s\ge0$ [box invariance];
> (ii) it converges to a critical point $\hat\theta$ of $F$, with $|d_i|\le\tilde d$ and $|v_i-a_i\Gamma(d_i)|\le E_v$ for all $i$, which is nondegenerate (Hessian $\succeq(\lambda_{\rm own}-H_{\rm c})I$ in the coordinates $(v,d)$), and which is the only critical point of $F$ in $B$;
> (iii) while $v_e<v_{\rm lo}$ the entrant's coefficient is strictly increasing; hence each level $\theta\le v_{\rm lo}$ is crossed exactly once and transversally;
> (iv) with $\Omega=0$ and all $K$ features fitted, $\hat\theta$ is the target (centres $c^*_k$, coefficients $A_k$, $F=0$).
> The smallest $\Delta$ for which a radius $r>d_0$ exists is $\Delta_{\rm entry}(K,R,d_0)$, tabulated in the report (Part A) with the conditions evaluated in ball arithmetic.

*Proof.* **Step 0 (the field).** For a unit $i$ let $\mathrm{rest}_i=\sum_{j\ne i}\epsilon_je_j+f_U$ ($-\sum_{\text{outside}}w_j\varphi_j$ included). From (1.1), with $\langle e_i,\varphi_i\rangle=a_i\Gamma(d_i)-v_i$,
$$-\partial_{v_i}F=a_i\Gamma(d_i)-v_i+\epsilon_iP_i,\quad P_i=\langle\mathrm{rest}_i,\varphi_i\rangle;\qquad -\partial_{c_i}F=-v_i\big[\psi_i(d_i)-\epsilon_iC_i\big],\quad \psi_i(d)=a_i\psi(d),\ C_i=\langle\mathrm{rest}_i,\partial_c\varphi_i\rangle.$$
**Step 1 (couplings).** Inside $B$ the distance from $c_i$ to $c_j,c^*_j$ is $\ge\ell$ and to $c^*_u$ ($u$ unfitted) is $\ge\Delta-r\ge\ell$; $|\langle\varphi_i,e_j\rangle|\le(a_j+v_j)\Gamma(\ell)$, $|\langle\partial_c\varphi_i,e_j\rangle|\le(a_j+v_j)\psi(\ell)$ (because $\Gamma$ and, beyond $\sqrt2$, $\psi$ decrease), and the units outside the box add at most $\Omega$ and $0.43\Omega$. Hence $|P_i|\le E_v$, $|C_i|\le E_c$ on $B$ (using $v_j\le\bar v$, $a_j\le R$, $K-1$ other features).
**Step 2 (faces).** Upper $v$-face $v_i=\bar v$: $-\partial_{v_i}F\le a_i-\bar v+E_v<0$ since $\bar v>R+E_v$ by construction. Lower $v$-face $v_i=v_{\rm lo}$ (or $v_0$ for the entrant): $-\partial_{v_i}F\ge a_i\Gamma(r)-v_{\rm lo}-E_v\ge\Gamma(r)-v_{\rm lo}-E_v\ge0$ with strict inequality for $v_0<v_{\rm lo}$ and for any $v_{\rm lo}<\Gamma(r)-E_v$; choosing $v_{\rm lo}$ slightly below $\Gamma(r)-E_v$ gives strict inward pointing. Centre faces $d_i=\pm r$: $\dot d_i=-\mu_cv_i[\psi_i(d_i)-\epsilon_iC_i]$ has the sign $\mp$ because $\psi_i(r)\ge\psi(r)>E_c\ge|C_i|$ by (C). Since the faces are strict and $M_i,\mu_c>0$ on $B$ ($v_i\ge\min(v_0,v_{\rm lo})>0$), $B$ is positively invariant (Nagumo); compactness gives (i).
**Step 3 (convergence).** $\dot F=-\sum_iM_i(\partial_{v_i}F)^2-\mu_c\sum_i(\partial_{c_i}F)^2\le0$ along (1.2), and $=0$ in $B$ only at critical points of $F$ ($M_i>0$ there). By LaSalle the $\omega$-limit set is a nonempty connected set of critical points of $F$ in $B$.
**Step 4 (location, nondegeneracy, uniqueness).** At a critical point in $B$: $\psi_i(d_i)=\epsilon_iC_i$, so $a_i\psi(|d_i|)\le E_c$, and $\psi(d)\ge\tfrac d2\Gamma(r)$ on $[0,r]$ gives $|d_i|\le\tilde d<r$; and $v_i=a_i\Gamma(d_i)-\epsilon_iP_i\in[\Gamma(\tilde d)-E_v,\ R+E_v]$. In $(v,d)$ the Hessian of $F$ is $\mathrm{blockdiag}(\begin{smallmatrix}1&s_i\\ s_i&t_i\end{smallmatrix})+H_{\rm cross}$ with $s_i=a_i\tfrac{d_i}2\Gamma(d_i)$, $t_i=v_ia_i(\tfrac12-\tfrac{d_i^2}4)\Gamma(d_i)$ (from $S_i$), and $\|H_{\rm cross}\|_2\le\|H_{\rm cross}\|_\infty\le H_{\rm c}$ (differentiate the cross terms of (1.1) twice; each entry is bounded by the same $\Gamma,\psi,|\Gamma''|$ at distance $\ge\ell$). The least eigenvalue of $\begin{pmatrix}1&s\\ s&t\end{pmatrix}$ increases with $t$ and decreases with $|s|$, so it is $\ge\lambda_{\rm own}$ on the convex set $D^*=\{|d_i|\le\tilde d,\ v_i\in[\Gamma(\tilde d)-E_v,R+E_v]\}$ (for $|d|\le\tilde d<\sqrt2$, $(\tfrac12-\tfrac{d^2}4)\Gamma(d)$ decreases and $\psi$ increases). Thus the Hessian is positive definite on $D^*$, all critical points of $B$ lie in $D^*$, so there is at most one, and it is nondegenerate. The $\omega$-limit set is a single point: (ii).
**Step 5 (monotone entry).** For the entrant, $\dot v_e=M_e(v_e)\,[a_e\Gamma(d_e)-v_e+\epsilon P_e]\ge M_e(v_e)[\Gamma(r)-E_v-v_e]>0$ for $v_e<v_{\rm lo}$: (iii). **Step 6.** If all features are fitted, $\mathrm{rest}_i$ contains no unfitted term and $F$ attains $0$ at $(a_k,c_k^*)\in D^*$; by uniqueness this is $\hat\theta$: (iv). \qed

*The entering orbit.* The entrant leaves $v=0$ only asymptotically: on the critical curve $\dot v=c\,v^{4/3}q$, so the orbit of the limit object has $v_e(s)\sim|s|^{-3}\to0$ as $s\to-\infty$, and it is the limit $s_0\to-\infty$ of the trajectories started at $(\hat\theta_k+O(v_0),\,v_0,\,d_0)$. Because the block's critical point is interior to $B$ ($|d_i|\le\tilde d<r$, $v_i\in(v_{\rm lo},\bar v)$), the coupling of the block to the entrant is $O(v_e)$ and the entrant's centre moves by $\int\mu_cv_e\,ds<\infty$, the point of the orbit at time $s_0$ is in $B$ for $s_0$ sufficiently negative **provided $r>d_0$ strictly** (the radius search of the script starts above $d_0$). By (i) it then stays in $B$ for $s\ge s_0$, for every such $s_0$, i.e. the whole entering orbit lies in $B$, and the $\omega$-limit argument of Steps 3--4 applies to it unchanged. (No statement is made about $s<s_0$.)

**Corollary A (H3 and the "exponentially small $\delta_0$").** In the continuum, for the limit object of Definition 1, whenever a box with $r>d_0$ exists at the entry (that is, (L),(C),(N) hold at $(\Delta,R,K,r)$; the smallest such $\Delta$ is $\Delta_{\rm entry}(K,R,d_0)$), the entry layer relaxes to the nondegenerate critical point $\hat\theta_k$ of the next block, within $\tilde d$ in the centres and $E_v$ in the coefficients of the decoupled block: (H3) and (H2) hold at that entry. **Proposition A's** threshold is explicit and does not depend on the length of the slide, on the mobility, the mass or the wall (this independence is a property of Prop. A only; see Theorem K for what else is used): the finite-horizon continuation of the sketch is replaced by the box, because the layer is a gradient flow (Lemma 1.1) and the product structure of (1.1) makes the faces' sign conditions controllable. $\delta_0$ of Lemma S2(iii)/S4 is, in the supplement's parametrisation $\delta=\Gamma(\Delta-r)$-overlap, the largest $\delta$ with (L),(C),(N).

*Remark 2.1 (what the proposition does not need).* It does not assume a decoupled solution, a linearisation, a small slide, an equilibrium at the start of the entrant (any $v_0\in(0,v_{\rm lo}]$), or that the entrant starts nearest to its feature (any $|d_e(0)|\le r$); it allows opposite signs, and any positive mobility. Its loss is the triangle inequality in the couplings (the factor $R+\bar v\le2R/(1-\kappa)$ bounds $a_j+v_j$ although $e_j\to0$ for a fitted unit) and the crude $H_{\rm cross}$ (condition (N) is the binding one at $d_0\ge0.4$; $\Delta_{\rm entry}$ is a conservative sufficient condition, not a transition: the inner problem converged to the target in every run with $\Delta\ge4\sigma$ and its only failures at 2--3$\sigma$ are premise failures, a weak feature entered first with no critical point near it; (L),(C) alone hold from $\Delta=4.24$ for $K=2,R=1,d_0=0.41$).

*Remark 2.2 (the network, $\varepsilon>0$; the dormant mass).* Lemma 1.1 holds for the $\varepsilon$-flow too ($W^\varepsilon$ strictly increasing). Hence (i) holds verbatim for the trained network's units in a box, with $\Omega=\sum_{\text{dormant}}|w_j|/a_{\min}$: **invariance needs only (L),(C)** (not (N), which is for uniqueness of the critical point). The largest tolerated $\Omega$, maximised over the radius $r>d_0=0.4167$ and evaluated in ball arithmetic (Part A, K=2): (L),(C) only: $0.69$ ($R=1,\Delta=6$), $0.58$ ($R=2.4,\Delta=6$), $0.71$ ($R=10,\Delta=7$), $0.82$ at large $\Delta$; with (N): $0.26,\,0.12,\,0.036$ (needlessly restrictive for invariance). So *no unit of a formed block can leave its box, and an entrant in its box cannot lose coefficient below $v_{\rm lo}$, while the dormant coefficients sum to less than $\Omega_{\max}^{LC}$.* This is not "free in $\varepsilon$": with the supplement's core formula $|w_j|\simeq\mathcal K^3\varepsilon^{3/2}\mu_j^{-3}$, $\mathcal K^3=(8/\pi^3)a_{\max}/(m\sqrt{2\cdot\gamma a_{\max}^2/4G})=0.096$ for the repository's law (an asymptotic core formula; I have not carried out its explicit remainder, which is (M2)), 20 dormant units give $\Omega\lesssim1.9\,\varepsilon^{3/2}\mu^{-3}/a_{\min}$, and $\Omega\le\Omega^{LC}_{\max}$ asks $\mu\gtrsim(1.9/\Omega^{LC}_{\max}a_{\min})^{1/3}\varepsilon^{1/2}\approx1.4\,\varepsilon^{1/2}$-$1.7\,\varepsilon^{1/2}$: the **same $\varepsilon^{1/2}$ scaling as the margin condition, with a constant comparable to $\xi_\theta\approx2$** (it does not dominate; it is of the same order). Numerically (Part H, $K=2$, 24 trained-network runs, $\varepsilon=10^{-4},10^{-6},10^{-8}$, $\Delta=6,8\sigma$): over the whole run both formed units stay inside the (L),(C)-box built for the observed $\Omega$ (largest total dormant $|w|$: $0.60\,a_{\min}$ at $\Delta=6,\varepsilon=10^{-4}$; $2.7\cdot10^{-1}$ at $10^{-6}$; $7.6\cdot10^{-5}$ at $10^{-8}$), 24/24; the ratio $|d|/r$ is 1.00 at the start because the box is the smallest admitted and the entrant starts at $d_0\approx r$. This gives the *location* of the layer's end state at every $\varepsilon$ in place of the near-field Gronwall estimate; it does not give the *timing* (previous section, (M1)-(M4)).
## 3. Theorem K (a computable certificate for the limit object) and its proof

The limit object (Definition 1) with equal walls $\rho=1$ on the starting kernels $c_1,\dots,c_J$. Define recursively, for $k=0,\dots,K$: a fitted set $S_k$ ($|S_k|=k$) with block state $\hat\theta_k$ the critical point of $F$ over the units of $S_k$ that lies in $D^*$ (Prop. A), residual $r_k=f^*-\sum_{i\in S_k}w_i\varphi(\cdot;\hat c_i)$, rates $p^k_j=\langle r_k\varphi_j\rangle$, duals $u^k_j$ at the time $\tau_k$ ($u^0=0$, $\tau_0=0$) and, for the inactive kernels,
$$\Delta t^k_j=\frac{1-\operatorname{sgn}(p^k_j)u^k_j}{|p^k_j|},\qquad j_{k+1}=\arg\min_j\Delta t^k_j,\quad \tau_{k+1}=\tau_k+\min_j\Delta t^k_j,\quad u^{k+1}_j=u^k_j+(\tau_{k+1}-\tau_k)p^k_j,$$
the entrant $j_{k+1}$ being assigned to the unfitted feature $f(k+1)$ nearest to its centre, at the offset $d_0^{k+1}=|c_{j_{k+1}}-c^*_{f(k+1)}|$.

> **Theorem K** (continuum Gram, equal walls $\rho_j=1$, finite generic checks (a),(b), and $\Delta\ge\max_k\Delta_{\rm entry}(K,R,d_0^k)$ with $d_0^k$ the $k$-th entrant's offset, the entrant being any starting kernel, not only the nearest: on 391 of the 1154 benchmark paths the entrant is not the nearest kernel and this is covered by the per-target check of $d_0^k$). Suppose that for each $k=0,\dots,K-1$: **(a)** the minimiser $j_{k+1}$ is unique ($\gamma_k=(\Delta t^{(2)}-\Delta t^{(1)})/\Delta t^{(1)}>0$) and $\operatorname{sgn}p^k_{j_{k+1}}=\operatorname{sgn}A_{f(k+1)}$; **(b)** $|u^{k+1}_j|<1$ for every inactive $j\ne j_{k+1}$ ($\mu_k=1-\max_j|u^{k+1}_j|>0$); **(c)** the conditions (L),(C),(N) of Proposition A hold for a radius $r\ge d_0^{k+1}$ at the separation $\Delta$ and the ratio $R$. Then the limit object has exactly $K$ entries, the kernels $j_1,\dots,j_K$ at the times $\tau_1<\dots<\tau_K$, with no exit; its blocks are the $\hat\theta_k$; its final block is the target with zero residual; (H1) holds with margin $\mu_{\min}=\min_k\mu_k$, (H2) with $\lambda_k\ge(\lambda_{\rm own}-H_{\rm c})\min(\min_{[v_{\rm lo},\bar v]}M_i,\mu_c)$, (H3) by Prop. A, and (H4) at every level $\theta<v_{\rm lo}a_{\min}$ (not above). Scope: Prop. A's independence of mobility, mass and walls does not carry over to Theorem K, which uses equal walls (unequal walls change the hitting times and the recursion), and (H2)'s rate $\lambda_k$ depends on the mobility. Conditions (a)--(c) are strict inequalities between explicit numbers: **they are checked by a finite computation on any given target** (the script's `certify`).

*Proof.* Induction on $k$. Before the $k+1$-st entry the block sits at $\hat\theta_k$ (inductive hypothesis; $\hat\theta_0$ is empty) and by Definition 1 the inactive duals integrate the constant rates $p^k_j$ (the residual is constant while the block is stationary), so each $u_j(\tau)$ is piecewise linear, $|u_j|$ is convex on each piece and its maximum on $[\tau_k,\tau_{k+1}]$ is at the end points; by (a),(b) at $\tau_k$ [inductive (b)] and $\tau_{k+1}$ no inactive dual other than $j_{k+1}$ reaches $\pm1$ on $[0,\tau_{k+1})$, and $j_{k+1}$ reaches it first, uniquely and transversally ($\gamma_k>0$, $p^k_{j_{k+1}}\ne0$). The entrant's coefficient has the sign of its dual $=\operatorname{sgn}A_{f(k+1)}$ [(a)], so the units' signs are those of Prop. A. By (c) and Prop. A (with the previous block at $\hat\theta_k\in D^*\subset B$, and the entrant started at $v_0\downarrow0$ at its lattice offset $d_0^{k+1}\le r$) the inner problem relaxes to the unique critical point $\hat\theta_{k+1}\in B$; during the relaxation (zero duration on the slow scale) the inactive duals do not move at leading order. No coefficient vanishes ($v_i\ge v_{\rm lo}>0$) so there is no exit, and the duals of the active units stay at their walls with the sign of their coefficients. After $K$ entries the residual is $0$ by Prop. A(iv); the duals stop; the path ends. (H4): Prop. A(iii). \qed

**Remark K2 (the decoupled-prediction regime; heuristic, not part of the theorem).** For $K=2$ with $o_k\le h/2$ the offset of feature $k$ from its nearest kernel, the decoupled hitting times are $T_k=1/(Z\,a_k\Gamma(o_k))$, and the recursion's entries are the decoupled kernels, in the order of $T_k$, as soon as every first-order correction (the tails $a_l\Gamma(\Delta-x)$ of the other feature at the kernels, and the error function $\epsilon_1(c)=A_1\Gamma(c-c_1^*)-\hat w_1\Gamma(c-\hat c_1)=O(E_v+\tilde d)$ of the fitted unit) is smaller than the decoupled margins $\mu_0,\varpi$: relative size $\sim R\,\Gamma(\Delta-h/2)/\Gamma(h/2)$ times a polynomial factor, i.e. $\Delta_{\rm dec}\approx h/2+2\sigma\sqrt{\ln(C/\min(\mu_0,\varpi))}$. I did not derive $C$; Section 4D measures $\Delta_{\rm dec}$ directly from the recursion (median constant $\min(\mu_0,\varpi)/\Gamma(\Delta_{\rm dec}-h/2)=0.8$, 10--90\% range $0.09$--$2.4$).

## 5. Point (4): do the mismatch margins scale like $\varepsilon^{1/2}$? -- No; the reviewer is right about the data.

**Correct statement:** the first-order theory predicts, per target, mismatch iff $\mu_j<\varepsilon^{1/2}(\mathrm{dev}_j+\xi_\theta)$ with $\xi_\theta=(\mathcal K^3/\theta)^{1/3}\approx2$ and $\mathrm{dev}_j$ target-dependent (four decades); the data do **not** show $\mu_{\rm crit}\propto\varepsilon^{1/2}$ in the tested range: the effective exponent is $\approx0.3$ ($0.26$-$0.31$), with a spread of $D_t$ over two decades. ## 6. What the skeleton computes (replacement for "closed-form skeleton")

`exp_transit_certificate.skeleton` runs the inverse scale space on the *starting kernels* with piecewise-linear duals: between entries every inactive dual grows at $\langle r_k\varphi_j\rangle$ (explicit), the next entry is the first dual to reach $\pm1$ (an arg-min), and after each entry the block's residual $r_k$ is replaced by
* (linear skeleton) $f^*-\Pi_kf^*$, $\Pi_k$ the orthogonal projection (a linear least-squares solve in the samples' inner product) onto $\mathrm{span}\{\varphi(\cdot;c^*_m),\nabla_c\varphi(\cdot;c^*_m)\}$ of the *true centres* of the fitted features;
* (fitted skeleton) the residual of the sign-constrained **nonlinear** least-squares fit (`polish_block`, trust-region reflective) of the fitted features' units, **started at the true centres and amplitudes**.
It stops when a kernel off the nearest set $E$ enters. Inputs: the data function $f^*$ on the samples, the true centres (and, for the fitted variant, amplitudes) of all features, the starting kernels. So it is an **oracle** (it needs the answer's centres), it is **not closed form** (a nonlinear least-squares solve per entry; only the duals and the arg-min are explicit), and its output is a predicted entry sequence / an exactness flag, not an estimator. Suggested wording: "the *oracle skeleton*: the piecewise-linear inverse scale space on the starting kernels in which, after each entry, the block is replaced by the least-squares fit of the fitted features' units started at the true features (one small nonlinear solve per entry; no training, no ODE; it needs the features' centres and amplitudes, so it is a predictor of the path, not a method)". The certificate of Theorem K is the same recursion *continued through intruders* (an entrant that is not the nearest kernel is assigned to the nearest unfitted feature and fitted); on the report's 1020 targets it lists the path's kernels, in order, on 1019 (the skeleton's list equals the path's only where no intruder enters, e.g. 60/60 from 5 sigma, 16/60 at 2 sigma). Beyond $\Delta_{\rm entry}$ it is not a predictor but, by Theorem K, the path.

## 7. What you can and cannot say (checklist)
* CAN: in the continuum, the coupled entry layer is proved for the limit object with explicit conservative thresholds ($\Delta_{\rm entry}$: 4.66$\sigma$ for $K=2$, $R=1$, $d_0=0.4167\sigma$; 5.50 at $R=2.4$; 5.08/5.84 for $K=3$; 6.70 at $R=10$); the path is then the recursion (Thm K, equal walls, finite generic checks); the network's units cannot leave the box for any $\varepsilon$ while the dormant mass is below $\Omega^{LC}_{\max}$.
* CAN: $\Delta_0$ is computed and inside the tested range for K=2 (4.3-5.8$\sigma$ over local data, median 5.1), *in the continuum*. For the repository's sampled path in its original window the theorem is supported by 128 interior targets (K=2, 5-8$\sigma$; the other 440 of the 568 continuum-certified have a feature within 3.5 widths of the edge); in the wider window of Part I by 337 interior targets (K=2: 167, K=3: 170).
* CAN: the unpolished flow (40/40, and 480/480 on the wider-window targets, with and without the polish) and the direct inner-problem integration (108 runs) agree with the proof's prediction; in the wider window 337 interior targets, including 170 with K=3, satisfy the theorem's conditions and none of them misbehaves.
* CANNOT: that the network follows the path with an explicit $\varepsilon_0$, that Thm 1 is proved (it is an outline), or that mismatch margins scale as $\varepsilon^{1/2}$.
* CANNOT: that edge targets are covered; that $n>1$ was run (planar: same proof checked by hand, no planar certificate); that $\Delta_{\rm entry}$ is a transition (it is not); that entries are the decoupled kernels beyond $\Delta_{\rm entry}$ (usually, not provably); that the agreement of the certificate with the polished path tests Prop. A.
