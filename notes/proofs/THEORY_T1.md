## 0. Status: what is proved, with which $\varepsilon_0$, and what is not

**Proved here (complete proofs below).** In the regime where the entry layers are controlled (continuum Gram, Gaussian features of the kernels' width, noiseless, equal masses $b_j=1$, $n=1$, separation $\Delta\ge\Delta_{T1}$ of Table 8.B: $4.9\sigma$ for $K=2$, $R=1$, $d_0=0.42\sigma$; $5.7\sigma$ at $R=2.4$; $7.0\sigma$ at $R=10$; $5.3$, $6.1$, $7.2\sigma$ for $K=3$):

* **Theorem T1** (§6, any $K$). For the limit object of Thm K (finite checks (a),(b)) and the paper's $P_0$ unit started at $\alpha=0$, $\nu^2=\varepsilon$: if an explicit finite system of inequalities $\mathcal S(\varepsilon)$ on the target holds, then on $[0,\lambda(1.05\tau_K+1)]$ (the horizon of the experiments) the network forms **exactly** the limit object's units $e_1,\dots,e_K$ and no other; each entrant's coefficient is nondecreasing until formed and stays above $a_{\min}v_{lo}$ (so (H4) holds for the network); the entry times are the limit's up to $O(\varepsilon^{1/2})$ in the slow time $\tau=t/\lambda$ with explicit constants; every inactive dual stays within $D_j/\lambda=O(\varepsilon^{1/2})$ of the limit dual; the block is within $O(\varepsilon^{3/2})$ of the limit's blocks outside the layers; the final fit is the target up to $O(\varepsilon^{3/2})$ (not the outline's $O(\varepsilon^{3/4})$). The four missing estimates of THEORY_NOTE are closed: **(M1)** outer-interval tracking = Lemma 4.1 (a Gershgorin / weighted-max-norm Lyapunov argument whose steady state contains no mobility) + Lemma 5.1; **(M2)** the explicit remainder of the dormant core formula = Lemma 2.2 (exact algebra from the closed form: $\kappa\tanh x=\cot(\vartheta+x/\kappa)$); **(M3)** the layer duration = Lemma 3.3 ($T_1$ by the exact dual coordinate $d\eta/dt\ge a_{\min}v_{lo}-W(\eta)$, $T_2,T_3$ by coordinate comparison inside the box; Lemma 4.2 is the Łojasiewicz/PL version with the Hessian bound $\nu=\lambda_{\rm own}-H_c$); **(M4)** summing the layers' deviations against the margins = the bootstrap of §6 (the recursion is triangular: layer $k$ needs only layers $<k$).
* **The threshold $\varepsilon_0$.** $\mathcal S(\varepsilon)$ is a finite computation (`cert`). For fixed target it holds for all small $\varepsilon$ (Cor. 7.1) and its first-order form is
$$\varepsilon_*=\Big(c_u\min\Big\{\min_j\frac{\mu_j}{D_j+s_\Omega},\ \min_k\frac{\tau_{k+1}-\tau_k}{\text{window}_k+\text{lead}_{k+1}},\ \frac{\tau_1}{\text{lead}_1}\Big\}\Big)^2,\qquad c_u=\frac{\pi a_{\max}}{2\sqrt2\,G\sqrt{v_s}}=7.025,$$
$\mu_j$ the dormant margins, $D_j$ the explicit dual-deviation bounds ($83$–$132$ in $\eta$ units for $R=1$, $315$–$500$ for $R=1.5$–$2.4$ on the test targets), $s_\Omega\approx35$ the distance at which the dormant mass $n\cdot33.3/s^3$ stays below $\Omega a_{\min}$. **Numbers** (Table 8.C, 15 continuum targets with $K=1,2,3$, $\Delta=6$–$10\sigma$): the certified $\varepsilon_0$ (largest half-decade from which $\mathcal S$ holds at every smaller one, refined by three further points below it) is $10^{-8}$–$3\cdot10^{-6}$ for the 13 targets with a relative gap $>0.01$ of the entry times ($10^{-10}$ and $10^{-8}$ for the two with gaps $3\cdot10^{-4},10^{-3}$, where the proof needs the layers' windows to be disjoint); the measured $\varepsilon_{\max}$ is $3\cdot10^{-7}$–$10^{-4}$ (median $3\cdot10^{-5}$; Part E of theory_h3: median $10^{-5}$). **The ratio $\varepsilon_{\max}/\varepsilon_0$ is 32 on 8 targets, 100 on 3, 316 on 2** (median 32, i.e. a factor 5.6 in $\varepsilon^{1/2}$), and $10^4,10^6$ on the two near-tie targets; on the repository's own sampled $P_0$ network (8 interior targets) $32$–$316$ (median 208). First-order $\varepsilon_*/\varepsilon_0\in[1.1,12]$ (median 6).
* **Verification.** 345 (target, $\varepsilon$) pairs with my integrator and 184 with the repository's $P_0$ code: **0 violations** (`cert` true $\Rightarrow$ the network forms the path's units). Every ingredient inequality of the proof was checked on 30 integrated trajectories at $\varepsilon_0$ and $\varepsilon_0/10$ (Table 8.D: actual/bound $\le0.53$ for the dual deviations, $\le0.91$ for the transit time, $\le0.55$ for the relaxation, $\le0.78$ for the entrants' lead; dormant mass $\le1.000\times$ the exact core bound).
* **What sets the measured threshold** (Table 8.H): at $\varepsilon_{\max}$ the margin in the dual coordinate $\lambda\mu_{\min}$ (with $\lambda=7.02\,\varepsilon^{-1/2}$) equals the *actual* dual deviation $E_{\rm act}$ of the dormant kernels (ratio median 1.33, range 0.92–1.86; $E_{\rm act}=43$–$66$, nearly independent of the target), and half a decade above it the ratio is $\le1.01$ (median 0.71) and the network no longer follows on 15 of 15 targets. The certificate asks $\lambda\mu\ge D+s_\Omega$ with $D/E_{\rm act}=1.5$–$9$ (median 2.4): this, and the half-decade grid, is the whole conservativeness.

**Not proved / not claimed.** (1) $n>1$. (2) $\Delta_{\rm entry}\le\Delta<\Delta_{T1}$ ($0.2$–$0.3\sigma$ wide): Prop. A holds and so does Lemma 3.1 (box, monotone entrants, once-transversal level crossing), but the tracking uses the diagonal dominance of the Hessian (two Gershgorin margins); the isotropic Lyapunov argument works under (N) alone with a forcing amplification $\sim10^{4}$–$10^{5}$ (§9). (3) "For **all** real $\varepsilon\le\varepsilon_0$" rests on the evaluation at a half-decade grid down to $10^{-14}$ with three further points below $\varepsilon_0$, and on the convergence argument of Cor. 7.1 below the grid — not on a computed bound at every $\varepsilon$ (monotonicity in $\varepsilon$ of each constant is not proved; Part C refinement: 45 of 45 pass). (4) The sampled window's edge, unequal masses, noise, the planar case, targets with $\Delta<\Delta_{T1}$. (5) The constants of `cert` other than Prop. A's are evaluated in double precision with 0.1 % margins on quadratures (ball arithmetic only for $v_{lo},E_v,E_c,\beta,\nu$), not interval arithmetic.

## 1. Setting and notation

**1.1 The network.** Lengths in widths $\sigma$; $\Gamma(d)=e^{-d^2/4}$, $\psi(x)=\frac x2\Gamma(x)$ for $x\ge0$ ($|\Gamma'(x)|=\psi(|x|)$, $\sup\psi=\psi(\sqrt2)=0.4289<0.43$), $|\Gamma''|(x)=|\frac{x^2}4-\frac12|\Gamma(x)\le\frac12$. Continuum Gram $\langle\varphi(\cdot;c)\varphi(\cdot;c')\rangle=Z\,\Gamma(c-c')$. Unit constants $(m,G,\gamma,a_{\max})=(12,1,0.1,1)$ for the repository, $v_s=\gamma a_{\max}^2/(4G)=0.025$, $\kappa=\sqrt{(2v_s-\varepsilon)/\varepsilon}$, wall $\lambda=\pi a_{\max}\kappa/(4Gv_s)=c_u\varepsilon^{-1/2}(1+O(\varepsilon))$, $c_u=7.025$, centre mobility $\mu_c=Gm/\sigma^2=833$. By Lemma S1 each unit is described by its dual coordinate $\eta_j$ and its centre; with $t'=Zt$ (the factor $Z$ only rescales time; "time" below is $t'$):
$$\dot\eta_j=g_j:=\sum_kA_k\Gamma(c_j-c_k^*)-\sum_iw_i\Gamma(c_j-c_i),\quad \dot c_j=\mu_c\,w_jh_j,\quad h_j:=\sum_kA_k\Gamma'(c_j-c_k^*)-\sum_{i\ne j}w_i\Gamma'(c_j-c_i),\quad w_j=W(\eta_j).\tag{1.1}$$
$W=W^\varepsilon$ is odd and strictly increasing; $\eta_j(0)=0$ ($\alpha=0$), $c_j(0)=c_j^0$. Target $f^*=\sum_{k\le K}A_k\varphi(\cdot;c^*_k)$, $a_k=|A_k|$, $a_{\min}=\min a_k$, $R=\max a_k/a_{\min}$, $\Delta=\min_{k\ne l}|c^*_k-c^*_l|$ ($=\infty$ for $K=1$); $\theta=\min\{0.01\max a_k,0.1a_{\min}\}$ is the repository's formation threshold.

**1.2 The limit object (Thm K data).** Recursion on the starting kernels, walls $1$ in $u=\eta/\lambda$, $\tau=t/\lambda$: block $k$ is the critical point $\hat y_k=(\hat w^{(k)},\hat c^{(k)})\in D^*$ of the fit over the first $k$ entrants; rates $p^k_j=\sum_mA_m\Gamma(c_j^0-c^*_m)-\sum_{i\le k}\hat w_i\Gamma(c^0_j-\hat c_i)$; entrant $e_{k+1}=\arg\min_j(1-\operatorname{sgn}(p_j^k)u_j)/|p^k_j|$ assigned to the nearest unfitted feature $f(k+1)$ at offset $d_0^{k+1}$; entry times $\tau_k$, $t_k=\lambda\tau_k$; duals piecewise linear. Put $p_k:=|p^{k-1}_{e_k}|$ (the entrant's rate on its last pre-entry interval), $\hat g^{(k)}(c):=\sum_mA_m\Gamma(c-c_m^*)-\sum_{i\le k}\hat w_i\Gamma(c-\hat c_i)$ (the limit's rate function at stage $k$; $\hat g^{(K)}\equiv0$: $\hat y_K$ is the target), $\mathrm{dist}_j(t)=\lambda(1-|u_j(t/\lambda)|)$ for a non-entrant, $\mu_j=\min_t\mathrm{dist}_j/\lambda$. Thm K's (a),(b) (unique minimiser with the feature's sign; $|u_j|<1$ at every entry time) and $r>d_0^k$ for all $k$ are assumed.

**1.3 Normalisation.** Prop. A has $a_{\min}=1$. Below $v'=v/a_{\min}$, $\bar v=a_{\min}\bar v'$ etc.; in $(v',d)$ coordinates the mobilities are $M(a_{\min}v')$ and $\mu_ca_{\min}^2$ (Lemma 1.1 of the note).

## 2. The unit (this is (M2))

**Lemma 2.1 (closed forms).** With $x=\alpha/a_{\max}\ge0$, $q(x)=\varepsilon+(2v_s-\varepsilon)\tanh^2x$:
$$w=\tfrac{a_{\max}}m\tanh x\,(\varepsilon+2v_s\sinh^2x),\quad \eta=\tfrac{a_{\max}}{2Gv_s}\big(x+\kappa\arctan(\kappa\tanh x)\big),\quad \tfrac{d\eta}{dx}=\tfrac{a_{\max}}{G\,q},\quad M:=\tfrac{dw}{d\eta}=\tfrac Gm\,q\,(q+4v_s\sinh^2x).$$
The distance to the wall is $s(x):=\lambda-\eta=\frac{a_{\max}\kappa}{2Gv_s}\big(\arctan\frac1{\kappa\tanh x}-\frac x\kappa\big)$, strictly decreasing from $\lambda$ ($x=0$) to $-\infty$; $w$ and $M$ are increasing in $x$ (on any coefficient interval $M$ is smallest at the lower end); $M(0)=G\varepsilon^2/m>0$.
*Proof.* $w=\beta a/m$, $\beta=\varepsilon+2v_s\sinh^2x$, $a=a_{\max}\tanh x$ (S1(i)); $\eta$ is S1(ii), $d\eta/dx=a_{\max}/(Gq)$ is its proof; $\arctan y=\frac\pi2-\arctan\frac1y$ and $\lambda=\frac{a_{\max}\kappa}{2Gv_s}\frac\pi2$ give $s(x)$. $dw/dx=\frac{a_{\max}}m(q+4v_s\sinh^2x)$ because $(\varepsilon+2v_s\sinh^2x)\operatorname{sech}^2x=q$; divide by $d\eta/dx$; both factors of $M$ increase. $\square$

**Lemma 2.2 (the dormant core with its explicit remainder).** Let $0<\varepsilon<2v_s$, $\mu\in(0,1]$, $\vartheta=\pi\mu/2$, $s_\varepsilon=\cot\vartheta/\kappa<1$, $\rho=2v_s/(2v_s-\varepsilon)$. If $|\eta|\le(1-\mu)\lambda$ then
$$|w|\ \le\ \frac{a_{\max}}m\,s_\varepsilon\Big(\varepsilon+\frac{2v_ss_\varepsilon^2}{1-s_\varepsilon^2}\Big)\ \le\ \frac{\rho^{3/2}}{1-s_\varepsilon^2}\,\frac{a_{\max}\varepsilon^{3/2}}{m\sqrt{2v_s}}\,\frac{\cos\vartheta}{\sin^3\vartheta}\ \le\ \frac{\rho^{3/2}}{1-s_\varepsilon^2}\,\frac{a_{\max}}{m\sqrt{2v_s}}\;\varepsilon^{3/2}\mu^{-3}.$$
Leading term: $\frac8{\pi^3}\frac{a_{\max}}{m\sqrt{2v_s}}\varepsilon^{3/2}\mu^{-3}=0.0963\,\varepsilon^{3/2}\mu^{-3}$ (the note's $\mathcal K^3$) times $(\vartheta/\sin\vartheta)^3\cos\vartheta\,\rho^{3/2}(1-s_\varepsilon^2)^{-1}=1+O(\mu^2,\varepsilon,\varepsilon\mu^{-2})$; the cruder last form has the constant $0.373$.
*Proof.* Put $\vartheta(x)=\pi s(x)/(2\lambda)=\frac\pi2-\arctan(\kappa\tanh x)-\frac x\kappa$ (Lemma 2.1: $2\lambda/\pi=a_{\max}\kappa/(2Gv_s)$); then **exactly** $\kappa\tanh x=\cot(\vartheta(x)+x/\kappa)$, the argument lying in $(0,\pi/2]$. For $|\eta|\le(1-\mu)\lambda$: $s\ge\mu\lambda$, $\vartheta(x)\ge\vartheta$, so $\kappa\tanh x\le\cot\vartheta$, i.e. $\tanh x\le s_\varepsilon$. $w$ increases with $\tanh x$ and $\sinh^2x=\tanh^2x/(1-\tanh^2x)$: first inequality. Insert $s_\varepsilon=\cot\vartheta\sqrt{\varepsilon/(2v_s-\varepsilon)}$: the first term is $A\rho^{1/2}\cot\vartheta$, the second $A\rho^{3/2}\cot^3\vartheta/(1-s_\varepsilon^2)$ with $A=a_{\max}\varepsilon^{3/2}/(m\sqrt{2v_s})$; their sum is $\le A\rho^{3/2}(1-s_\varepsilon^2)^{-1}\cot\vartheta(1+\cot^2\vartheta)$ and $\cot\vartheta(1+\cot^2\vartheta)=\cos\vartheta/\sin^3\vartheta$. Last: $\cos\vartheta\le1$, $\sin\vartheta\ge\frac2\pi\vartheta=\mu$. $\square$

**Corollary 2.3 (distance form).** Let $B(s):=W(\lambda-s)$ ($=\sup|W|$ over $|\eta|\le\lambda-s$), decreasing in $s$. As $\varepsilon\to0$ it converges to $B_0$, $s=\frac{a_{\max}}{2Gv_s}(\coth x-x)$, $B_0=\frac{2v_sa_{\max}}m\tanh x\sinh^2x$, and $B_0(s)s^3\to4/(G\gamma^2m)$ as $s\to\infty$: **a dormant unit at distance $s\gg a_{\max}/(2Gv_s)=20$ from its wall carries the coefficient $\simeq33.3\,s^{-3}$, whatever $\varepsilon$** (Table 8.A: $B s^3=33.32$ at $\mu=0.02$, $\varepsilon=10^{-8}$). *Proof.* $\kappa(\arctan\frac1{\kappa\tanh x}-\frac x\kappa)\to\coth x-x$; for large $s$, $x\simeq a_{\max}/(2Gv_ss)$ and $w\simeq\frac{2v_sa_{\max}}mx^3=\frac{a_{\max}^4}{4G^3v_s^2m\,s^3}$, with $v_s^2=\gamma^2a_{\max}^4/(16G^2)$. $\square$

**Lemma 2.4 (mass identity).** $\int_{\eta_1}^{\eta_2}W\,d\eta=\Psi(x_2)-\Psi(x_1)$, $\Psi(x)=\frac{a_{\max}^2\sinh^2x}{2Gm}$. *Proof.* S1(iii): $(\Psi^*)'=W$, $\Psi^*=(\beta-\varepsilon)/(\gamma m)=2v_s\sinh^2x/(\gamma m)$, $2v_s/\gamma=a_{\max}^2/(2G)$. $\square$ Hence along a linear piece of the limit's dual, at speed $p$ from distance $s_a$ to $s_b$: $\int B\,dt=|\Psi(s_b)-\Psi(s_a)|/p$ exactly.

## 3. The box for the network (this is (M3), first half)

**3.0 Box data (Prop. A with a dormant budget).** For $K,R,\Delta$, a radius $r<\sqrt2$ with $\ell:=\Delta-2r\ge\sqrt6$ ($K\ge2$) and $\Omega\ge0$ let $\kappa_c=(K-1)\Gamma(\ell)<1$ and, in units of $a_{\min}$,
$$\bar v=\tfrac{R(1+\kappa_c)+\Omega}{1-\kappa_c},\quad E_v=\kappa_c(R+\bar v)+\Omega,\quad E_c=(K-1)(R+\bar v)\psi(\ell)+0.43\Omega,\quad v_{lo}=\Gamma(r)-E_v,\quad \tilde d=\tfrac{2E_c}{\Gamma(r)}.$$
Fix $\hat d=\tilde d+0.05(r-\tilde d)$ and $\varsigma>0$ ($0.05$ in `cert`, $0.01$ in Table 8.B; the difference moves $\Delta_{T1}$ by $\le0.1\sigma$) with $\Gamma(\hat d)-E_v-\varsigma>0$. The relaxation set is $\hat D=\{|d|\le\hat d,\ v'\in[\Gamma(\hat d)-E_v-\varsigma,\ R+E_v+\varsigma]\}\supset D^*$ (convex). The cross-Hessian row sums (the two terms whose maximum is $H_c$ of the note) and the Gershgorin margins are
$$\mathrm{row}_v=(K-1)\big[\Gamma(\ell)+(R+\bar v)\psi(\ell)+\bar v\psi(\ell)\big]+0.43\Omega,\quad \mathrm{row}_d=(K-1)\big[(R+\bar v)\psi(\ell)+\bar v(R+\bar v)|\Gamma''|(\ell)+\bar v\psi(\ell)+\bar v^2|\Gamma''|(\ell)\big]+0.43\Omega+\tfrac12\bar v\Omega,$$
$$\beta_v=1-s_{\max}-\mathrm{row}_v,\qquad \beta_d=t_{\min}-s_{\max}-\mathrm{row}_d,\qquad s_{\max}=R\psi(\hat d),\ t_{\min}=(\Gamma(\hat d)-E_v-\varsigma)(\tfrac12-\tfrac{\hat d^2}4)\Gamma(\hat d).$$
**Regime (R):** (L) $v_{lo}>0$; (C) $\psi(r)>E_c$; (N) $\tilde d<r$ and $\lambda_{\rm own}(\hat d)>H_c$; $\beta_v,\beta_d>0$. Evaluated in ball arithmetic (`box_t1`). $\Delta_{T1}(K,R,d_0)$ is the smallest $\Delta$ with a radius $r>d_0$ for which (R) holds (Table 8.B); $\Delta_{T1}\ge\Delta_{\rm entry}$ because of the margins $\beta$ and of evaluating (N) at $\hat d>\tilde d$.
*Row sums.* The cross terms of $F$ are $\epsilon_i\epsilon_j\langle e_i,e_j\rangle$, $\langle e_i,e_j\rangle=a_ia_j\Gamma(c^*_i-c^*_j)-a_iv_j\Gamma(c^*_i-c_j)-v_ia_j\Gamma(c_i-c^*_j)+v_iv_j\Gamma(c_i-c_j)$. The entries in the rows of $v_i$ and $d_i$ are bounded by $\Gamma(\ell)$, $(a_j+v_j)\psi(\ell)$, $v_j\psi(\ell)$ and $v_i(a_j+v_j)|\Gamma''|(\ell)$, $v_iv_j|\Gamma''|(\ell)$ ($\psi$ decreases beyond $\sqrt2$, $|\Gamma''|$ beyond $\sqrt6$, all distances $\ge\ell$), which gives the two sums; each dormant unit adds $0.43|w_l|$ (and $\frac12\bar v|w_l|$ to $\mathrm{row}_d$). The finite-difference Hessian on random points of the box stays below $0.6$ of the bounds (12000 points; the test uses 150).

**Lemma 3.1 (box invariance for the network; monotone entrants).** Let $e_1,\dots,e_K$ be distinct kernels, $e_k$ at the offset $d_k(0)$, $|d_k(0)|<r$, from the distinct feature $f(k)$; $w_k=\epsilon_kv_k$, $\epsilon_k=\operatorname{sgn}A_{f(k)}$, $d_k=c_{e_k}-c^*_{f(k)}$; $\mathcal N$ the other kernels. Assume on $[0,T]$ the **dormant budget** (DM) $\sum_{l\in\mathcal N}|w_l|\le\Omega a_{\min}$. Under (L), (C), $\kappa_c<1$, $\ell\ge\sqrt6$, for all $t\le T$ and all $k$:
(a) $0\le v_k\le a_{\min}\bar v$ and $|d_k|\le u_k:=\max(|d_k(0)|,\tilde d)<r$, with $\frac d{dt}|d_k|\le-\mu_cv_ka_{\min}\frac{\Gamma(r)}2(|d_k|-\tilde d)$ whenever $|d_k|\ge\tilde d$;
(b) $\epsilon_kg_k\ge a_{\min}v_{lo}-v_k$ and $\epsilon_kg_k\le a_k-v_k+a_{\min}E_v$; hence $\dot v_k>0$ while $v_k<a_{\min}v_{lo}$: **the entrant's coefficient is nondecreasing until it first reaches $a_{\min}v_{lo}$ and is $\ge a_{\min}v_{lo}$ afterwards** (each level $\vartheta\le a_{\min}v_{lo}$ is crossed once and transversally: (H4));
(c) $\dot d_k=-\mu_cv_k[a_k\psi(d_k)-\epsilon_kC_k]$ with $|C_k|\le a_{\min}E_c$, and $\epsilon_kg_k=a_k\Gamma(d_k)-v_k+P_k$ with $|P_k|\le a_{\min}E_v$.
*Proof.* For a box unit $i$ put $\mathrm{rest}_i=\sum_{j\ne i}\epsilon_je_j-\sum_{l\in\mathcal N}w_l\varphi_l$, $e_j=a_j\varphi^*_j-v_j\varphi_j$ (every feature has its box unit from $t=0$, so there is no unfitted term; an entrant with $v_j=0$ has $e_j=a_j\varphi_j^*$). From $F=\frac12\langle(\sum\epsilon_ie_i-\sum_lw_l\varphi_l)^2\rangle$: $\epsilon_ig_i=a_i\Gamma(d_i)-v_i+P_i$, $\dot d_i=-\mu_cv_i[a_i\psi(d_i)-\epsilon_iC_i]$, $P_i=\epsilon_i\langle\mathrm{rest}_i,\varphi_i\rangle$, $C_i=\langle\mathrm{rest}_i,\partial_c\varphi_i\rangle$ (the note's Step 0). While the box constraints hold, the distance from $c_i$ to $c_j$ and to $c^*_j$ is $\ge\ell$, so $|\langle\varphi_i,e_j\rangle|\le(a_j+v_j)\Gamma(\ell)$, $|\langle\partial_c\varphi_i,e_j\rangle|\le(a_j+v_j)\psi(\ell)$, and the dormant units add $\le\Omega a_{\min}$, $0.43\Omega a_{\min}$: $|P_i|\le a_{\min}E_v$, $|C_i|\le a_{\min}E_c$. Faces of $\mathcal B=\{0\le v_i\le a_{\min}\bar v,|d_i|\le r\}$: on $v_i=a_{\min}\bar v$, $\epsilon_ig_i\le a_{\min}(R+E_v-\bar v)<0$ (definition of $\bar v$); on $v_i=0$ (the start), $\epsilon_ig_i\ge a_{\min}(\Gamma(r)-E_v)>0$; on $|d_i|=r$, $\frac d{dt}|d_i|\le-\mu_cv_ia_{\min}(\psi(r)-E_c)<0$ by (C). The coefficient's mobility is $M>0$ for $\varepsilon>0$ ($M(0)=G\varepsilon^2/m$), $\dot v_i=M\epsilon_ig_i$, so the faces are strict and $\mathcal B$ is positively invariant (Nagumo: every face, hence every corner, points inward). (a), (c) and the first half of (b) follow, using $\psi(d)\ge\frac d2\Gamma(r)$ on $[\tilde d,r]$. For $v_k<a_{\min}v_{lo}$: $\epsilon_kg_k\ge a_{\min}(\Gamma(r)-E_v)-v_k>0$ since $a_k\Gamma(d_k)\ge a_{\min}\Gamma(r)$; this also gives $\epsilon_k\eta_k>0$ for $t>0$. $\square$

**Lemma 3.2 (the relaxation set is invariant).** Under the assumptions of 3.1 each unit separately keeps $(v_i,d_i)\in\hat D$ once it is there: on $|d_i|=\hat d>\tilde d$, $\frac d{dt}|d_i|\le-\mu_cv_ia_{\min}(\psi(\hat d)-E_c)<0$; on $v_i=a_{\min}(\Gamma(\hat d)-E_v-\varsigma)$ with $|d_i|\le\hat d$, $\epsilon_ig_i\ge a_{\min}\varsigma>0$; on $v_i=a_{\min}(R+E_v+\varsigma)$, $\epsilon_ig_i\le-a_{\min}\varsigma$. $\square$

**Lemma 3.3 (the layer: transit, centre, settling).** Assume (DM) and 3.1 on $[0,T]$. Fix $\delta_1\in\mathbb R$; $x_1=x(s=\delta_1)$, $w_1=W(x_1)$, $v_\#=\frac12a_{\min}v_{lo}$, assume $w_1<v_\#$. For the entrant $e=e_k$ let $t_a=\inf\{t:|\eta_e|\ge\lambda-\delta_1\}$ (there $v_e=w_1$) and $t_1=\inf\{t:v_e\ge v_\#\}$. If $t_a\le T$:
1. $t_a<\infty$ and $\int_0^{t_a}v_e\,dt\le\mathrm{pre}:=\Psi(x_1)/(a_{\min}v_{lo}-w_1)$;
2. $t_1-t_a\le T_1:=\int_{x_1}^{x_\#}\frac{d\eta/dx}{a_{\min}v_{lo}-w(x)}dx$ and $\int_{t_a}^{t_1}v_e\,dt\le A_{\rm tr}:=\int_{x_1}^{x_\#}\frac{w(x)\,d\eta/dx}{a_{\min}v_{lo}-w(x)}dx$, $w(x_\#)=v_\#$;
3. for $t\ge t_1$: $v_e\ge v_\#$ and $|d_e|\le\hat d$ for $t\ge t_2$, $t_2-t_1\le T_2:=\kappa_d^{-1}\ln^+\frac{u-\tilde d}{\hat d-\tilde d}$, $\kappa_d=\mu_cv_\#a_{\min}\frac{\Gamma(r)}2$, $u=\max(\max_kd_0^k,\tilde d)$;
4. with $V_t=a_{\min}(\Gamma(\hat d)-E_v)$, $v_{up}=a_{\min}(R+E_v)$, $m_V=M(v_\#)$, $m_V'=M(v_{up})$: for $t\ge t_2$, $(V_t-v_e)_+\le(V_t-v_\#)e^{-m_V(t-t_2)}$ and $(v_e-v_{up})_+\le(a_{\min}\bar v-v_{up})e^{-m'_V(t-t_2)}$; so $(v_e,d_e)\in\hat D$ for all $t\ge t_{rel}$, $t_{rel}-t_2\le T_3:=\max\big\{m_V^{-1}\ln^+\frac{V_t-v_\#}{a_{\min}\varsigma},m_V'^{-1}\ln^+\frac{a_{\min}\bar v-v_{up}}{a_{\min}\varsigma}\big\}$, and $\int_{t_2}^{t_{rel}}|v_e-\hat v|\le\mathrm{Env}:=\frac{V_t-v_\#}{m_V}+\frac{a_{\min}\bar v-v_{up}}{m'_V}+a_{\min}(R+2E_v-\Gamma(\hat d))T_3$ for any $\hat v$ in the coefficient range of $D^*$. Total: $t_{rel}-t_a\le T_{\rm lay}:=T_1+T_2+T_3$.
*Proof.* 1. $\eta_e$ increases at the rate $\epsilon g\ge a_{\min}v_{lo}-v_e$ while $v_e<a_{\min}v_{lo}$ (3.1(b)); if it never reached $\lambda-\delta_1$ then $v_e\le w_1<v_\#$ and $\dot\eta\ge a_{\min}v_{lo}-w_1>0$ forever, absurd. For $t<t_a$, $\dot\eta\ge g_-:=a_{\min}v_{lo}-w_1$ and $\int_0^{t_a}v\,dt=\int W\,d\eta/\dot\eta\le\Psi(x_1)/g_-$ (Lemma 2.4). 2. On $[t_a,t_1]$, $dt\le d\eta/(a_{\min}v_{lo}-W(\eta))$ and $v\,dt\le W\,d\eta/(\cdots)$; substitute $\eta=\eta(x)$. 3. After $t_1$, $v_e\ge v_\#$ (3.1(b)) and 3.1(a) gives $|d|-\tilde d\le(u-\tilde d)e^{-\kappa_d(t-t_1)}$. 4. After $t_2$, $\epsilon g\ge a_{\min}\Gamma(\hat d)-v-a_{\min}E_v=V_t-v$, so $Y=V_t-v_e$ obeys $\dot Y=-M(v_e)\epsilon g\le-M(v_e)Y\le-m_VY$; above $v_{up}$, $\epsilon g\le v_{up}-v$. The integral is $\int Y+\int Y^++$ the width of the coefficient range of $\hat D$ times $T_3$. Once in $\hat D$ the unit stays (3.2). $\square$

## 4. Stability of the formed block (this is (M1), first half)

Let the first $k$ box units be in $\hat D$ on $I=[t_0,t_1]$ and let $\hat y=\hat y_k\in D^*\subset\hat D$ be the critical point of the fit $F'^{(k)}_0$ of the target by these $k$ units, in the normalised coordinates $y=(v'_i,d_i)_{i\le k}$. With $\tilde e(t)$ the effect of the other units,
$$\dot y_j=-m_j(t)\big(\partial_jF'^{(k)}_0(y)+\tilde e_j(t)\big),\qquad m_{v'}\ge M(a_{\min}v'_{\hat D,lo})>0,\quad m_d=\mu_ca_{\min}^2,\tag{4.1}$$
$\tilde e_{v'_i}=\epsilon_ia_{\min}^{-1}\sum_{l\notin\text{block}}w_l\Gamma(c_i-c_l)$, $\tilde e_{d_i}=\epsilon_iv'_ia_{\min}^{-1}\sum_{l\notin\text{block}}w_l\Gamma'(c_i-c_l)$; $|\tilde e_{v'}|\le\Gamma(\ell)\sum_{\text{future entrants}}\frac{w_l}{a_{\min}}+\Omega'$, $|\tilde e_d|\le\bar v'\psi(\ell)\sum_{\rm future}\frac{w_l}{a_{\min}}+0.43\bar v'\Omega'$, $\Omega'=\sum_{l\in\mathcal N}|w_l|/a_{\min}$ (future entrants are $\ge\ell$ away from the block).

**Lemma 4.1 (weighted-max-norm Lyapunov).** Let $\kappa_*:=\min\{M(a_{\min}v'_{\hat D,lo})\beta_v,\ \mu_ca_{\min}^2\beta_d\}$, $V=\max_j|y_j-\hat y_j|$, $\bar e_{v'}(t):=\Gamma(\ell)\sum_{\text{future}}w_l(t)/a_{\min}+\Omega'_{pk}$, $\bar e_d(t):=\bar v'\psi(\ell)\sum_{\text{future}}w_l(t)/a_{\min}+0.43\bar v'\Omega'_{pk}$ (the bounds of (4.1) with the dormant mass replaced by its peak) and $V_\infty(t):=\max\{\bar e_{v'}/\beta_v,\bar e_d/\beta_d\}$, which is **nondecreasing** on $I$ (a constant plus nondecreasing coefficients of units not yet formed: 3.1(b)). Then
$$V(t)\le V_\infty(t)+\big(V(t_0)-V_\infty(t_0)\big)_+e^{-\kappa_*(t-t_0)},\qquad \int_{t_0}^tV\le\frac{V(t_0)}{\kappa_*}+\int_{t_0}^tV_\infty,\qquad V(t_0)\le V_{\rm diam}:=\max\{2\hat d,\ \text{width of the $v'$-range of }\hat D\}.$$
*Proof.* $\partial_jF'_0(y)=\sum_i\bar H_{ji}(t)z_i$, $z=y-\hat y$, $\bar H=\int_0^1\nabla^2F'_0(\hat y+\tau z)d\tau$ ($\hat D$ is convex). On $\hat D$: $\bar H_{v_iv_i}=1$ exactly (cross terms are linear in $v_i$); $|\bar H_{v_id_i}|\le s_{\max}$; $\bar H_{d_id_i}\ge t_{\min}-(\text{its cross part})$ ($\partial^2_dS_i=v_ia_i(\frac12-\frac{d^2}4)\Gamma(d)$ is $\ge t_{\min}$); the cross part of each row is $\le\mathrm{row}_v,\mathrm{row}_d$. At a coordinate $j$ attaining $V=|z_j|$ (Dini derivative): $D^+V\le-m_j(\bar H_{jj}|z_j|-\sum_{i\ne j}|\bar H_{ji}||z_i|-|\tilde e_j|)\le-m_j\beta_jV+m_j|\tilde e_j|\le-\kappa_*(V-V_\infty)$ whenever $V\ge V_\infty$ ($|\tilde e_j|\le\beta_jV_\infty$). $\Phi=V_\infty+ce^{-\kappa_*(t-t_0)}$, $c=(V(t_0)-V_\infty(t_0))_+$, satisfies $\Phi'\ge-\kappa_*(\Phi-V_\infty)$ because $V_\infty$ is nondecreasing; the comparison for $D^+V\le-\kappa_*(V-V_\infty)$ on $\{V\ge V_\infty\}$ (and $V<V_\infty\le\Phi$ elsewhere) gives $V\le\Phi$. Integrate. $V(t_0)\le V_{\rm diam}$ because $y,\hat y\in\hat D$. $\square$
*Why this form.* The steady state $V_\infty$ contains only the margins $\beta$, never the mobilities (the centres are $\mu_ca_{\min}^2/M\approx5\cdot10^3$ times faster than the coefficients). The isotropic estimate below gives $\dot q\le-\kappa q+(m_{hi}H_{up}/\nu)|\tilde e|$ and inflates every forcing term by that factor.

**Lemma 4.2 (the Łojasiewicz / PL form: exponential rate under (N) alone).** On $\hat D$ the Hessian of $F'^{(k)}_0$ satisfies $\nu I\preceq\nabla^2F'_0\preceq H_{up}I$, $\nu=\lambda_{\rm own}(\hat d)-H_c$, $H_{up}=\max(1,\frac R2\bar v)+s_{\max}+H_c$. Unforced ($\tilde e=0$): with $\mathbb M=\mathrm{diag}(m_j)\succeq m_{lo}I$, $\frac d{dt}F'_0=-\nabla F'^T\mathbb M\nabla F'\le-m_{lo}|\nabla F'|^2\le-2m_{lo}\nu(F'_0-F'_0(\hat y))$, so $F'_0-F'_0(\hat y)\le(F'_0-F'_0(\hat y))(t_0)e^{-2m_{lo}\nu(t-t_0)}$ and $\|y-\hat y\|_2\le\sqrt{H_{up}/\nu}\,\|y(t_0)-\hat y\|_2\,e^{-m_{lo}\nu(t-t_0)}$. Numbers ($K=2$, $R=1$, $\Delta=8$, $r=0.5$, $\Omega=0.02$, $a_{\min}=0.7$): $\nu=0.464$, $m_{lo}=M(0.68)=0.068$, rate $m_{lo}\nu=0.032$ against $\kappa_*=0.066$ of Lemma 4.1 (time constants $31$ and $15$ in $t'$; on the slow scale $0.032\cdot$ is $O(1)$ in $t$, hence $O(\varepsilon^{1/2})$ in $\tau$). This is the exponential rate the outline asked for; its drawback is the forced response (previous paragraph).

## 5. Dual deviations (this is (M1), second half)

For a kernel $j$ let $\bar u_j(t)$ be the limit's dual in $\eta$ units; for the entrant $e_k$ extended after $t_{k-1}$ linearly with its rate $p_k$ (so $\bar u_{e_k}(t_k)=\lambda$ and the extension is the dual the limit object would have had without entering). $E_j:=\eta_j-\bar u_j$. Let $\mathrm{st}(t)$ be the number of limit entries $\le t$ (capped at $k-1$ for $j=e_k$); then $\dot{\bar u}_j=\hat g^{(\mathrm{st})}(c_j^0)$.

**Lemma 5.1 (decomposition).** $\dot E_j=[\hat g^{(\mathrm{st})}(c_j)-\hat g^{(\mathrm{st})}(c^0_j)]+R_j$,
$$R_j=-\sum_{i\le K}\big[w_i\Gamma(c_j-c_i)-\hat w_i\Gamma(c_j-\hat c_i)\big]-\sum_{l\in\mathcal N}w_l\Gamma(c_j-c_l),\qquad |R_j|\le\sum_i\big[\omega^v_{ji}|v_i-\hat v_i|+\omega^d_{ji}\min(v_i,\hat v_i)|d_i-\hat d_i|\big]+\Omega_{ne}(t),$$
$\hat w_i=\hat w_i^{(\mathrm{st})}$ ($=0$ for $i>\mathrm{st}$). Weights: for a non-entrant $j$ with $|c_j-c_j^0|\le d_c$, $\omega^v_{ji}=\Gamma(X_{ji})$, $\omega^d_{ji}=\psi_{\sup}(X_{ji})$, $X_{ji}=(|c_j^0-c^*_{f(i)}|-r-d_c)_+$; for the entrant $e_k$: $X_{e_k,k}=0$, $X_{e_k,i}=(|c^*_{f(k)}-c^*_{f(i)}|-2r)_+$; $\psi_{\sup}(x)=\sup_{y\ge x}\psi(y)$. *Proof.* $g_j=\sum_mA_m\Gamma(c_j-c^*_m)-\sum_iw_i\Gamma(c_j-c_i)-\sum_{l\in\mathcal N}w_l\Gamma(c_j-c_l)$ and $\hat g^{(\mathrm{st})}(c_j)=\sum_mA_m\Gamma(c_j-c^*_m)-\sum_i\hat w_i\Gamma(c_j-\hat c_i)$. Two splittings give $|w\Gamma-\hat w\hat\Gamma|\le|w-\hat w|\max(\Gamma,\hat\Gamma)+\min(w,\hat w)\sup|\Gamma'||c-\hat c|$; all $c_i,\hat c_i$ lie within $r$ of $c^*_{f(i)}$. $\square$

**Lemma 5.2 (non-entrants: mass and drift; uses (M2)).** Assume $|E_l|\le D_l$ on $[0,T]$ for the non-entrants. Then $|w_l(t)|\le B(\mathrm{dist}_l(t)-D_l)$ (monotonicity of $W$), $\Omega_{ne}(t)\le\sum_lB(\mathrm{dist}_l-D_l)$, and over the limit's linear pieces $\int B\,dt$ is given exactly by Lemma 2.4: $\int_0^T\Omega_{ne}\le\mathrm{mass}:=\sum_l\int B$. The centre moves by $|\dot c_l|\le\mu_c|w_l||h_l|$, $h_l=\hat g^{(\mathrm{st})\prime}(c_l)+\partial_cR_l$, with $|\hat g^{(\mathrm{st})\prime}|\le H_l^{(\mathrm{st})}$ (sup over $|c-c^0_l|\le d_c$, grid plus Lipschitz margin) and $\int|\partial_cR_l|\le\sum_i[\psi_{\sup}(X_{li})T^{v,{\rm tot}}_i+G2_{\sup}(X_{li})T^{d,{\rm tot}}_i]+0.43\,\mathrm{mass}$ ($G2_{\sup}(x)=\sup_{y\ge x}|\Gamma''(y)|$; totals from 5.4). Hence $|c_l-c_l^0|\le\mathrm{dcb}_l(t):=\mu_c(\int^tH_l^{(\mathrm{st})}B\,dt'+\mathrm{peak}_l[\ldots])$ and the drift-induced dual deviation is $\le\int\min\{\mathrm{osc}_l^{(\mathrm{st})},H_l^{(\mathrm{st})}\mathrm{dcb}_l\}dt$ ($\mathrm{osc}$ = oscillation of $\hat g^{(\mathrm{st})}$ on $|c-c_l^0|\le d_c$). In the last stage $H^{(K)}\equiv0$ ($\hat g^{(K)}\equiv0$: after the last entry a displaced kernel feels no force and acquires no drift-induced deviation, the target and the block cancelling identically). $\square$

**Lemma 5.3 (entrants: the shift, (M1) for the entry times).** At $t_a=t_a^k$: $\eta_{e_k}(t_a)=\lambda-\delta_1$, so $E^{\rm ext}_{e_k}(t_a)=-\delta_1-p_k(t_a-t_k)$, i.e.
$$t_a-t_k=\frac{-\delta_1-E^{\rm ext}_{e_k}(t_a)}{p_k}\in\Big[-\frac{\delta_1+D^e_k}{p_k},\ \frac{D^e_k-\delta_1}{p_k}\Big]=:[-S_k^-,S_k^+]\quad\text{if }|E^{\rm ext}_{e_k}(t_a)|\le D^e_k,$$
$$D^e_k=\Big[\textstyle\sum_{k'<k}(\text{layer terms of 5.4 with the entrant-kernel weights})\Big]+\sum_{i\ge k}\omega^v_{e_ki}\,\mathrm{pre}+\mathrm{mass}+\mathcal D^{\rm pre}_k,\qquad \mathcal D^{\rm pre}_k=\frac1{g_-}\int_0^{x_1}\min\{\mathrm{osc}_k,H_k\Delta c(x)\}\frac{d\eta}{dx}dx,$$
$\Delta c(x)=\min\{2r,\mu_c(H_k+h_{dev})\Psi(x)/g_-\}$: the **pre-layer drift of the entrant's centre toward its feature** (large: $w_e$ is not tiny before $t_a$ and $\mu_c=833$) — bounded by the oscillation of the limit's rate function over the positions $|d|\le u_k$ and its Lipschitz constant times the displacement $\mu_c\int w_e|h_e|\le\mu_c(H_k+h_{dev})\Psi(x)/g_-$ (Lemma 2.4, $\dot\eta\ge g_-$). For any level $\vartheta\le v_\#$ with $\delta_\vartheta=s(W^{-1}\vartheta)$ the crossing time satisfies $|t_\vartheta-t_k|\le(|\delta_\vartheta|+D^e_k)/p_k+T_1$. *Proof.* Lemma 5.1 integrated up to $t_a$ with $dt=d\eta/\dot\eta$, Lemma 3.1(b), Lemma 3.3. $\square$

**Lemma 5.4 ($L^1$ deviations of the units).** Let $S_k=\max(S^+_k,S^-_k)$, $W_k=(|\delta_1|+D^e_k)/p_k+T_{\rm lay}$ (the window of layer $k$ is contained in an interval of this length), and
$$\mathcal V:=\frac{V_{\rm diam}}{\kappa_*}+c_F\frac{K\,\mathrm{pre}}{a_{\min}}+c_N\,\Omega'_{pk}\,T_{\rm fin},\qquad c_F=\frac{\Gamma(\ell)}{\beta_v}+\frac{\bar v'\psi(\ell)}{\beta_d},\quad c_N=\frac1{\beta_v}+\frac{0.43\bar v'}{\beta_d}$$
(Lemma 4.1 integrated). Then for the unit $k$ (with $\hat v_k$ its coefficient in its own block)
$$\int|v_k-\hat v_k|\le T^v_k:=\mathrm{pre}+A_{\rm tr}+\hat v_kT_1+a_{\min}\bar v'S_k+a_{\min}\bar v'T_2+\mathrm{Env}+a_{\min}\mathcal V,$$
$$\int\min(v_k,\hat v_k)|d_k-\hat d_k|\le T^d_k:=(r+\tilde d)A_{\rm tr}+\hat v_k(r+\tilde d)T_2+2\hat d\,\hat v_kT_3+\hat v_k\mathcal V+(r+\tilde d)w_1S^+_k,$$
and a unit $i<k$ already in $\hat D$ contributes during the window of $k$: $\int|v_i-\hat v_i|\le W^D_vW_k+a_{\min}\mathcal V$, $\int\min|\Delta d|\le2\hat d\,\hat v_iW_k+\hat v_i\mathcal V$ ($W^D_v$ the width of the coefficient range of $\hat D$; $v_i$ and $\hat v_i$ both lie in it, $|d_i-\hat d_i|\le2\hat d$).
*Proof.* Let $H=\mathbb 1_{t\ge t_k}$ (the limit's coefficient is $\hat v_kH$). On $[0,t_a)$: $|v-\hat vH|\le v+\hat vH$, $\int v\le\mathrm{pre}$ and $\int\hat vH=\hat v(t_a-t_k)_+\le\hat vS^+_k$. On $[t_a,t_1)$: $\le v+\hat vH$, i.e. $A_{\rm tr}+\hat vT_1$. On $[t_1,\infty)$ with $t<t_k$ (the network ahead of the limit): $|v|\le a_{\min}\bar v'$ on a set of measure $\le(t_k-t_1)_+\le(t_k-t_a)_+\le S^-_k$; with $t\ge t_k$: on $[t_1,t_2]$ at most $a_{\min}\bar v'T_2$, on $[t_2,t_{rel}]$ at most Env (3.3(4)), from $t_{rel}$ on $\le a_{\min}V$ and $\int V\le\mathcal V$ (4.1). If $t_a\ge t_k$ the second set is empty, if $t_a<t_k$ the first term vanishes: only one of $S^\pm$ occurs, hence the maximum. The $d$-part is the same case analysis with $\min(v,\hat v)\le v$ and $|d-\hat d|\le r+\tilde d$ (before $t_2$), $\le2\hat d$ (on $[t_2,t_{rel}]$), $\le V$ afterwards; it vanishes for $t<t_k$ because $\hat v=0$. Old units stay in $\hat D$ (3.2) and the limit's block is in $D^*\subset\hat D$. $\square$

## 6. Theorem T1 and its proof (this is (M4))

**The system $\mathcal S(\varepsilon;\pi)$.** Parameters $\pi=(r,\Omega,\delta_1,\hat d\text{-fraction},\varsigma,d_c)$ and the horizon $T_{\rm fin}=\lambda(1.05\tau_K+1)$. With the constants of 3.0 (regime (R)), Lemmas 2.1, 3.3, 4.1 and the limit data of 1.2, let $D_j>0$ ($j\in\mathcal N$) satisfy
$$\mathfrak E_j(D):=\sum_{k=1}^K\big[\omega^v_{jk}T^v_k+\omega^d_{jk}T^d_k\big]+\sum_{k=1}^K\sum_{i<k}\Big[\big(\omega^v_{ji}W^D_v+\omega^d_{ji}2\hat d\hat v_i\big)W_k+\big(\omega^v_{ji}a_{\min}+\omega^d_{ji}\hat v_i\big)\mathcal V\Big]+\mathrm{mass}(D)+\mathrm{drift}_j(D)\ \le\ D_j,\tag{6.1}$$
(mass and drift evaluated with $\mathrm{dist}_l-D_l$ in $B$, Lemma 5.2; $D^e_k$, $S_k$, $W_k$ from 5.3, 5.4), and
$$\text{(S1) }\Omega'_{pk}:=\max_t\sum_lB(\mathrm{dist}_l-D_l)/a_{\min}\le\Omega;\quad\text{(S2) }\max_t\mathrm{dcb}_l\le d_c;\quad\text{(S3) }w_1<v_\#;\tag{6.2}$$
$$\text{(S4) }t_1\ge\tfrac{(\delta_1+D^e_1)_+}{p_1},\ \ t_{k+1}-t_k\ge\tfrac{(\delta_1+D^e_{k+1})_+}{p_{k+1}}+\tfrac{(D^e_k-\delta_1)_+}{p_k}+T_{\rm lay};\qquad\text{(S5) }\max_{l,t}B(\mathrm{dist}_l-D_l)<\theta<a_{\min}(\Gamma(\hat d)-E_v-\varsigma),\ \theta<v_\#.\tag{6.3}$$

**Theorem T1.** Let the data satisfy 1.1–1.2 (Thm K's (a),(b), signs, $r>d_0^k$, regime (R)). If $\mathcal S(\varepsilon;\pi)$ holds then on $[0,T_{\rm fin}]$ the network (1.1) satisfies:
1. **(no other unit; box)** $|E_l|\le D_l$, $|w_l|\le B(\mathrm{dist}_l-D_l)<\theta$, $\sum_{\mathcal N}|w_l|\le\Omega a_{\min}$, $|c_l-c_l^0|\le d_c$ for the non-entrants; each entrant stays in the box of 3.1, is nondecreasing until it reaches $a_{\min}v_{lo}$ and stays above it; $\{j:|w_j|>\theta\}=\{e_1,\dots,e_K\}$ from the time all entrants have crossed $\theta$ on;
2. **(entry times)** $t_a^k-t_k\in[-S_k^-,S_k^+]$; the entrant is in $\hat D$ from $t_a^k+T_{\rm lay}$ on; every level $\vartheta\le a_{\min}v_{lo}$ (in particular $\theta$) is crossed at $\tau_k+O(\varepsilon^{1/2})$, within $(\max(S^\pm_k)+|\delta_\vartheta|/p_k+T_{\rm lay})/\lambda$ (5.3);
3. **(duals)** $|u_j^{\rm net}(\tau)-u_j^{\rm lim}(\tau)|\le D_j/\lambda=O(\varepsilon^{1/2})$ for all non-entrants on $[0,\tau_{\rm end}]$;
4. **(blocks)** from $\max(t_k,t^k_{rel})$ to the next window, $\max_j|y_j-\hat y_{k,j}|\le V_\infty+V_{\rm diam}e^{-\kappa_*(t-t_{rel})}$ with $V_\infty=O(\varepsilon^{3/2}\mu^{-3})$ outside the next entrant's pre-layer: the final $K$ units are within $O(\varepsilon^{3/2})$ of the target in coefficients and centres;
5. **(hypotheses)** the network satisfies the analogues of (H1) (no exit: $v_k\ge a_{\min}v_{lo}$ after formation; margins $\ge\mu_j-D_j/\lambda$), (H3) (the layer is Lemma 3.3 for the network itself), (H4) (3.1(b)), (H2) ($\nu>0$ on $\hat D$).

*Proof.* Let $T^*$ be the supremum of $T\le T_{\rm fin}$ with $|E_l|\le D_l$ on $[0,T]$ for all $l\in\mathcal N$ ($T=0$: $E=0$). On $[0,T^*)$: (S1) and Lemma 5.2 give (DM) with budget $\Omega a_{\min}$ and $|w_l|<\theta$; (S2) gives $|c_l-c_l^0|\le d_c$; so Lemmas 3.1–3.3 hold on $[0,T^*)$. **Induction on $k$** (the system is triangular): assume the layer statements for $k'<k$ (window length $\le W_{k'}$, the $L^1$ bounds of 5.4). At the layer start, $|E^{\rm ext}_{e_k}(t_a^k)|\le D^e_k$ by Lemmas 5.1–5.3: only layers $<k$ contribute, plus the masses and the pre-drift, all bounded independently of layer $k$; hence $t_a^k\in[t_k-S_k^-,t_k+S_k^+]$ and, by (S4), $t_a^k$ comes after the end $\le t_{k-1}+S^+_{k-1}+T_{\rm lay}$ of the previous window and the windows do not overlap — this is exactly (S4). The entrant passes through the phases of 3.3 within $T_{\rm lay}$, then the whole block is in $\hat D$ and Lemma 4.1 applies up to the next window (the forcing is nondecreasing: the next entrants' coefficients are, by 3.1(b); the non-entrants' mass enters through its peak). This is 2 for layer $k$ and gives 5.4 for the units $\le k$. Therefore, for every non-entrant $j$ and $t<T^*$, Lemmas 5.1, 5.2, 5.4 give $|E_j(t)|\le\mathfrak E_j(D)\le D_j/1.0001$ (the factor is the strictness of the computation). By continuity of $E_j$ the bound persists beyond $T^*$, so $T^*=T_{\rm fin}$. 1 and 3 follow; 4 is Lemma 4.1 with $V_\infty$ on the plateau; 5 as said. $\square$

## 7. Corollaries

**Corollary 7.1 (existence of $\varepsilon_0$, scaling, rates).** For fixed data and $\pi$ every constant of $\mathcal S$ other than the non-entrants' mass and drift converges as $\varepsilon\to0$ (the unit's functions $W_\varepsilon,s_\varepsilon,M_\varepsilon$ converge uniformly on compacts of $x$ to their $\kappa=\infty$ forms, Cor. 2.3; $T_1,A_{\rm tr},\mathrm{pre},T_2,T_3,\mathrm{Env},\kappa_*$ are integrals/maxima of them; the limit data are $\varepsilon$-free in $\tau$). The mass and drift terms are $O(\varepsilon)$ and $O(\varepsilon^{1/2})$ ($\mathrm{dist}_l\propto\lambda\propto\varepsilon^{-1/2}$, $B\sim33s^{-3}$). ## 9. What fails for the general statement, why, and what was tried

**The statement is narrower than Theorem 1 in four ways; each has a precise cause.**

1. *Separation.* $\Delta\ge\Delta_{T1}$, which exceeds $\Delta_{\rm entry}$ by $0.2$–$0.3\sigma$ (8.B). Below it the first condition to fail is $\beta_d=t_{\min}-s_{\max}-\mathrm{row}_d>0$ (diagonal dominance of the centre rows, Lemma 4.1); (N) survives a little longer. For $\Delta_{\rm entry}\le\Delta<\Delta_{T1}$ what is still proved is Lemma 3.1 (box invariant for the network, entrants monotone, levels crossed once, the layer's end state located). What is lost is the forced tracking: the isotropic argument (Lemma 4.2, needs (N) only) has the forcing coefficient $m_{hi}H_{up}/(\nu\kappa)$ of order $10^4$–$10^5$ against $\approx1/\beta$ for Lemma 4.1; in an early version of the script this made $D\approx2\cdot10^4$ instead of $\approx10^2$ and the certificate held only below $10^{-13}$. Below $\Delta_{\rm entry}$ even (H3) is unproved (the inner problem still converges numerically down to $2$–$3\sigma$ except when the first unit has no critical point).
2. *Dimension.* Every step is dimension-free (offsets enter radially, cross terms through $\Gamma,\psi,|\Gamma''|$ at distance $\ge\ell$) **except** the Hessian row sums and the own block, checked for $n=1$ only. For $n>1$ the own block has a tangential direction with eigenvalue larger than the radial one (the note's hand check); not run here.
3. *All $\varepsilon\le\varepsilon_0$.* A computed bound at every real $\varepsilon\le\varepsilon_0$ would need the monotonicity in $\varepsilon$ of $T_1,A_{\rm tr},\mathrm{pre},\mathrm{Env},\kappa_*$ (they depend on $\varepsilon$ through $\kappa$ in $s(x)$, $W$; $O(\varepsilon)$ corrections); not proved. The grid check (down to $10^{-14}$, 45/45 refinements) and Cor. 7.1 replace it.
4. *Model.* Continuum Gram (Part E uses interior targets; the window's edge is outside, as in the note), equal masses (unequal masses change the walls $\lambda_j=\lambda\rho_j$ and the recursion; the same proof goes through with $\mathrm{dist}_j=\lambda_j-|\eta_j|$ but code and Thm K data assume equal walls), noiseless data, the limit's finite checks (a),(b).

**Why $\varepsilon_0$ is below the measured threshold.** See 8.H and the last paragraph of §8: the proof pays $D+s_\Omega$ where the network pays $E_{\rm act}$. In $D$ the entrant's own window dominates ($40$–$92\%$): $\hat v\,T_1$ (the worst-case drive $a_{\min}v_{lo}-w$ instead of the actual one), $\bar v\max S^\pm$ (the full coefficient over the whole shift, not the profile $v_e(t)$), the settling Env. A sharper rigorous estimate would use the actual drive along the transit (a Riccati comparison) and the monotone profile in the shift window.

**Attempts that did not work (each fixed the final form).** (i) *One global dual bound for all kernels and layers*: the window is $\propto D/p_k$ and each layer adds $\bar\Theta W_k$ to $D$, so the fixed-point map has slope $\sum_k\bar\Theta/p_k>1$: no solution; the triangular recursion and per-kernel $D_j$ remove it. (ii) *Box widths for the old units* ($\bar v-v_{lo}$, $2r$) instead of the width of $\hat D$ ($\approx0.1$): a factor $\approx4$ in the old-unit term. (iii) *A constant centre-force bound* ($0.43\sum a_k$) for the dormant kernels: drift $>10^3$ and a certificate failing above $10^{-9}$ for a target measured at $10^{-5.5}$; the stage-resolved force of the limit's residual (zero in the last stage), the oscillation cap and the window gating bring it to $\le10\%$ of $D$. (iv) *The isotropic stability estimate*: item 1. (v) *Bounding $\int w$ over the approach by the final coefficient*: replaced by the exact mass identity (Lemma 2.4). (vi) *Merging the bound with a time-resolved Gronwall in the near field* (the outline): exponentially small $\varepsilon_0$; replaced by the box, which holds at any $\varepsilon$.

**What the dormant centres do (an observation).** A dormant kernel at distance $s$ from its wall carries $w\simeq33/s^3$, but its centre moves at $\mu_cw\,h$ with $\mu_c=833$: a kernel with margin $\lambda\mu\approx100$ drifts by $\sim0.1\sigma$ toward the feature it neighbours, its rate increasing. This is the mechanism behind the mismatches near the threshold (note, Sec. 5); in the proof it is the term "drift" of 5.2 — $\le10\%$ of $D$ at $\varepsilon_0$, but the reason why the certificate's $D$ rises steeply within a decade above $\varepsilon_0$.

Since the margin law is $\lambda\mu\ge D+s_\Omega$, i.e. $\varepsilon_0\approx(c_u\mu/(D+s_\Omega))^2$, the threshold decays like $K^{-2}$ at fixed margin $\mu$ from this term alone; and $D/E_{\rm act}$, the proof's conservativeness, grows from $1.5$-$9$ (median 2.4, $K\le3$) to $2.7$-$25$ (median 8.6, $K=4..6$), which is the reason for the ratio $316$ against $32$. The reason is the crude $L^1$ bound for each old unit's residual transient (Lemma 5.4: $\sum_i\omega_{ji}(T^v_i+T^d_i)$, each unit paid at its worst case, not at its actual, mostly cancelling, effect).

**What was not achieved for $K\ge4$:** a uniform-in-$K$ lower bound on $\varepsilon_0$ (it depends on the target through $\mu,\ $the gaps and $D$, and no statement for random targets is claimed); targets with $K>6$ were not run against the network (only the regime table above).

### 12.2 The plane, $n=2$ (item 2)

**Setting.** Centres $c\in\mathbb R^2$, features $c^*_k\in\mathbb R^2$, $\Gamma(x)=e^{-|x|^2/4}$ (radial), $\nabla\Gamma(x)=-\tfrac x2\Gamma$, $|\nabla\Gamma|=\psi(|x|)$, $\nabla^2\Gamma(x)=\Gamma(|x|)\big(\tfrac{xx^{\!\top}}4-\tfrac I2\big)$ with eigenvalues $(\tfrac{|x|^2}4-\tfrac12)\Gamma$ (radial) and $-\tfrac12\Gamma$ (tangential). Dynamics (1.1) with $\dot c_j=\mu_c\,w_jh_j$, $h_j\in\mathbb R^2$, i.e. **scalar** centre mobility, as in the repository's isotropic unit. The offsets $d_j=c_j-c^*_{f(j)}\in\mathbb R^2$; $d_0$ is now the distance of the entrant to the nearest feature, $\le h/\sqrt2$ on a square lattice.

**Which steps use $n=1$, and the hand proof for $n=2$.**

*(a) Box (Lemma 3.1).* Only radial quantities enter. With $\rho=|d|$, $\dot\rho=\langle d/\rho,\dot d\rangle=-\mu_cv[a\psi(\rho)-\epsilon\langle d/\rho,C\rangle]$ where $C_i=\langle\mathrm{rest}_i,\partial_c\varphi_i\rangle\in\mathbb R^2$; by $|\langle\varphi_i,\partial\varphi_j\rangle|$-type bounds $|C_i|_2\le(a_j+v_j)\psi(\ell)$ summed over $j$ (the norm of $\nabla\Gamma$ is $\psi$ in any dimension) $+0.43\Omega$: the same $E_c$. $P_i$ is a scalar, the same $E_v$. The faces $v=0$, $v=a_{\min}\bar v(1+0^+)$, $\rho=r$ are as in 1-D. So $\bar v,E_v,E_c,v_{lo},\tilde d$ are **unchanged**; (L), (C) unchanged. The only 1-D shortcut is "$\psi(d)\ge\frac d2\Gamma(r)$ for $d\in[\tilde d,r]$": valid with $d\to\rho$.

*(b) Row sums.* The cross blocks of the Hessian of $F$ with respect to $(v_i,d_i)$, $(v_j,d_j)$ ($i\ne j$, $d\in\mathbb R^2$) are $1\times1$, $1\times2$, $2\times1$ and $2\times2$ matrices. Their *operator norms* are bounded by the same 1-D expressions because $\|\nabla\Gamma(x)\|_2=\psi(|x|)$ and $\|\nabla^2\Gamma(x)\|_{op}=\max\{|\tfrac{|x|^2}{4}-\tfrac12|,\tfrac12\}\Gamma(|x|)=|\Gamma''|(|x|)$ for $|x|\ge2$ (the radial eigenvalue dominates the tangential $\Gamma/2$ as soon as $|x|^2/4-1/2\ge1/2$), in particular for $|x|\ge\ell\ge\sqrt6$, where $|\Gamma''|$ is decreasing. Hence the block row sums $\mathrm{row}_v,\mathrm{row}_d$ of 3.0 hold verbatim with the dormant contributions $0.43|w_l|$, $\frac12\bar v|w_l|$ replaced by $\|\nabla\Gamma\|$-, $\|\nabla^2\Gamma\|$-sups over $|x|\ge$ the dormant units' distance (computed by `G2_sup2`, which includes $\Gamma/2$).

*(c) Own block.* $\partial^2_{d}(\text{own term})=v\,a\,(\tfrac I2-\tfrac{dd^{\!\top}}4)\Gamma(|d|)$: eigenvalues $v a(\tfrac12-\tfrac{|d|^2}{4})\Gamma$ (radial) and $v a\tfrac12\Gamma$ (tangential); the radial one is the smaller, so $\lambda_{\min}(T)\ge t_{\min}$ of 3.0 as in 1-D (for the units in $\hat D$: $|d|\le\hat d$, $v\ge\ldots$). The own $(v_i,d_i)$ coupling is the vector $a\nabla\Gamma(d)$ of norm $\le a\psi(|d|)\le s_{\max}$. (N) in the form $\lambda_{\rm own}>H_c$ also unchanged.

*(d) Lemma 4.1 in the plane.* Use the **block max norm** $V=\max_j\max\{|z_{v_j}|,|z_{d_j}|_2\}$. At a block attaining the max with $z_{d_j}\ne0$, $u=z_{d_j}/|z_{d_j}|$: since the mobility of $d_j$ is the *scalar* $\mu_ca_{\min}^2$,
$$\tfrac{d}{dt}|z_{d_j}|=\langle u,\dot z_{d_j}\rangle=-\mu_ca_{\min}^2\Big(\langle u,\bar H_{d_jd_j}u\rangle|z_{d_j}|+\langle u,\bar H_{d_jv_j}\rangle z_{v_j}+\sum_{i\ne j}\langle u,\bar H_{d_j,i}z_i\rangle+\langle u,\tilde e_{d_j}\rangle\Big)\le-\mu_ca_{\min}^2\Big(\lambda_{\min}(\bar H_{d_jd_j})V-s_{\max}V-\mathrm{row}_dV-|\tilde e_{d_j}|\Big),$$
which is the 1-D inequality with $\beta_d$ unchanged; the row of $v_j$ is the same with operator norms. The Dini-derivative comparison is identical. (With a *matrix* mobility the direction $u$ would rotate and the argument would need the symmetric part; the isotropic unit avoids this.) $V_{\rm diam}=\max\{2\hat d,\ \text{width}\}$ is unchanged (Euclidean diameter of the disc).

*(e) Layer lemma 3.3 and Lemmas 2.x.* The entrant's dual coordinate dynamics is scalar; $T_1,A_{\rm tr},{\rm pre}$ unchanged; $T_2$ uses $\rho$.

*(f) Dual deviations (5.1-5.4) and the limit object.* The deviation of a dormant kernel's dual from the limit's is driven by $\hat g^{(k)}(c)=\sum_mA_m\Gamma(|c-c^*_m|)-\sum_{i\le k}\hat w_i\Gamma(|c-\hat c_i|)$ and its gradient, evaluated on the disc $|c-c_j^0|\le d_c$: the sup and oscillation are now taken over a **disc** (`rgs2`), the Hessian-of-$\Gamma$ supremum includes the tangential $\Gamma/2$ (`G2_sup2`). The limit recursion (Thm K) is in terms of critical points of $F$ over the first $k$ entrants: `crit_block2`. Finite checks (a), (b) are as before (checked numerically for each target by `limit_path2`).

**Conclusion.** The box constants, the regime and $\Delta_{T1}$ are the same as for $n=1$, with the lattice offset $d_0\le h/\sqrt2$: $\Delta_{T1}(K=2)=4.9\,/\,5.8\,/\,7.2\,\sigma$, $(K=3)=5.3\,/\,6.1\,/\,7.5$ for $R=1,2.4,10$ at $d_0=0.4167$; at $d_0=0.59$: $5.2/6.1/7.6$ and $5.6/6.4/7.9$. **Theorem T1 holds for $n=2$** (hand proof (a)-(f); the verification of $\mathcal S$ is `cert2d`, the source of `cert` with Euclidean geometry substituted).

*Numerical check of (b)-(d).* Finite-difference Hessians on random points of the relaxation set (3 cases $K=2,3$; $\le200$ points each): (cross operator-norm row sums)/$\mathrm{row}_v,\mathrm{row}_d$ $\le0.02$; the block-Gershgorin margins exceed $\beta_v,\beta_d$ by $\ge0.026,\ 0.101$ (so the bounds used are not violated).

*Numbers: `cert2d` against the integrated planar network, 9 targets* ($K=1$ ×3, $K=2$ ×3, $K=3$ ×3; $\Delta=8.2$-$10.3$; dictionary: square lattice of spacing $h$ restricted to **radius $3.5\sigma$ of a feature**, 55-168 kernels). $\varepsilon_0=10^{-6}$-$10^{-9}$, measured $\varepsilon_{\max}=10^{-4}$-$10^{-6}$, ratio $100$-$1000$ (decade grid $10^{-3},\dots,10^{-11}$, so quantised to 10), $D=82$-$690$. **0 violations in 81 pairs.** Ingredients on 18 trajectories ($\varepsilon_0,\varepsilon_0/10$): dual deviation $\le0.50\,D$, transit $\le0.90\,T_1$, entrant lead $\le0.51$, shifts/monotone entrants/offsets/coefficients/formed set 18/18, dormant mass $\le0.95$ of the bound, final coefficient error $\le2.6\cdot10^{-7}$.

**Not covered in the plane:** (i) the dictionary is truncated to $3.5\sigma$ around the features; kernels farther away (which in a full plane window would number $O(\text{area}/h^2)$) are absent; they have small rates and large margins but this is not verified, so "planar" here means this finite dictionary; (ii) the same continuum-Gram, equal-mass, noiseless assumptions; (iii) $\mathcal S$ in the plane is evaluated in double precision (the ball-arithmetic cover of 12.3 is for $n=1$, $K\le3$).

### 12.3 Rigorous arithmetic (item 3)

**Method: find in floating point, verify in balls.** `theory_t1b_rigorous.py` re-evaluates $\mathcal S(\varepsilon;\pi)$ with python-flint balls (`arb`, 100 bits, outward rounding), for an *interval* of $\varepsilon$ at once. The floating-point certificate supplies the candidate (parameters $\pi$ and the dual bounds $D_j$, enlarged by a factor $1.1,1.5,2,4$); the ball code proves the inequalities $\mathfrak E_j(D)\le D_j$ etc. for the candidate, for every real $\varepsilon$ in the ball. Since only the *check* has to be rigorous, no ball-valued fixed point is needed.

What is recomputed in balls (all of them were in doubles with 0.1 % margins before; the box constants $v_{lo},E_v,E_c,\beta,\nu$ already were in balls):
* the unit: $\kappa,\lambda$ as balls of $\varepsilon$; $s(x)$ in the form $\frac{a_{\max}}{2Gv_s}(g(y)/\tanh x-x)$, $g(y)=\arctan y/y$ (series for $y<0.5$) so that a ball for $\varepsilon$ does not blow up; $x(s)$, $x(w)$ by bisection with three-valued comparisons (a ball is accepted only if the comparison is decided); $z^2$ of a ball containing $0$ as a nonnegative ball (a bug found here: arb's `z**2` can have a negative lower end and made $1/q(x)$ NaN for $\varepsilon<10^{-19.6}$);
* the layer constants $T_1,A_{\rm tr},\mathrm{pre}$ (Riemann **upper** sums of the monotone integrands on the exact grid of $x$, so each sum is a rigorous upper bound), $T_2,T_3,\mathrm{Env}$, $T_{\rm lay}$;
* $\sup$ and oscillation of $\hat g^{(k)}$ and of its derivative on the interval $[c^0_j-d_c,c^0_j+d_c]$ (subinterval balls, `rig_rgs`: 60 pieces with the derivative bound on each);
* the dormant mass by the $\Psi$-identity of Lemma 2.4 along the $\lambda$-free ratio $d\tau/(|u_a|-|u_b|)$; the sum over kernels of the per-kernel peaks for $\Omega'_{pk}$ (the "dormant-sum peak"); right-endpoint drift sums; the fixed point $\mathfrak E_j(D)\le D_j$; the order condition (S4) (with $\lambda(\tau_{k+1}-\tau_k)$), (S1), (S2), (S5).
* $x_{\rm of\_s}$ is the inversion the referee flagged; $\hat g$ and the drift sums use balls for the point $c_j^0$ (exact floats).

**Result (`theory_t1b.md`, Part Q): 10 targets, $n=1$, $K\le3$ (the dormant dictionary and the limit object of 8.C).** For **8 of the 10** targets the ball-arithmetic check holds on a chain of balls that covers **every real $\varepsilon\in[10^{-24},\,10^{\rm top}]$**, with
$$\mathrm{top}=-7.5,\,-5.5,\,-6.5\ (K=1);\quad -7.5,\,-8.0,\,-7.0\ (K=2,R=1);\quad -8.5\ (K=3,R=1);\quad -7.0\ (K=2,R=2.4),$$
i.e. $\mathrm{top}$ equals the floating-point $\varepsilon_0$ of 8.C on one target and is one half-decade ($\times3.2$) below it on seven (the same candidate $D$ must pass with the ball-valued margins and the 0.1 % safety factors become the actual enclosures); $17$-$122$ balls, $34$-$674$ verify calls per target; in the worst case $R=2.4$ the balls near the top must be narrow ($1/256$ decade) because the window starts depend sharply on $\varepsilon$. The two others: $K=2$, $R=2.4$, seed 14: the chain closes down to $10^{-7.09}$ only (the interval test fails at ball width $1/512$ decade just below the top; the float $\varepsilon_0=10^{-6}$); $K=3$, $R=1.5$, seed 15: covers $[10^{-19.1},10^{-8}]$ and then the 240 s budget per attempt ran out (not a failed test). Individually: at the grid points $\varepsilon=10^{-9},10^{-14}$ etc. the check holds for all tested targets in narrow balls.

**So for these 8 targets $\mathcal S(\varepsilon)$ is an interval-arithmetic statement for every real $\varepsilon\in[10^{-24},10^{\rm top}]$.** Combined with §6 (and the Nagumo/Dini arguments, which are by hand), Theorem T1 is proved for those targets on that range of $\varepsilon$ — **modulo the items below.** The number of balls is finite, so this *also* settles the "all $\varepsilon\le\varepsilon_0$" question on $[10^{-24},\varepsilon_{\rm top}]$ without monotonicity (12.4).

**What remains non-rigorous (exactly).**
1. **The limit object's data are floating-point inputs taken as exact:** the solutions $\hat y_k$ of the critical-point equations, the entry times $\tau_k$, the duals $u_j(\tau)$ at the breakpoints, the rates $p_k$, the margins $\mu_j$, the target. Each is the output of `limit_path` (`hybr` root finder, tolerance $10^{-14}$, plus the constructive recursion) and carries an error $\sim10^{-12}$ relative that is *not* enclosed. Enclosing it requires a ball Newton (Krawczyk) step for each $\hat y_k$ and the same for the entry-time recursion; not done. The thresholds (a), (b) of Thm K and the sign/assignment decisions (which dormant kernel enters, nearest feature) are decided on floats with the margins of Table 8.C (entry-time gaps as listed there).
2. The candidate $D_j$ is a float; fine (it is only verified), but **the formulas of `rigorous.verify` are a second implementation of `cert`**: they agree with the floating-point `cert` to about $0.1$ % on 5 targets (ball bound $\ge$ float value, `Etot`); I did not prove the transcription correct beyond that.
3. **Range of $\varepsilon$:** $[10^{-24},10^{\rm top}]$ only; for $\varepsilon<10^{-24}$ (and for the two incomplete targets, on the uncovered part) nothing rigorous. $K\ge4$, the plane, the sampled window are in doubles only.
4. python-flint/Arb correctness, and the hand proofs of §§2-6 (not formalised).
5. Within the verifier: the sub-interval grids for $\sup/\mathrm{osc}$ of $\hat g$ use the derivative bound of $\hat g$ on each piece computed in balls (rigorous), but the number of pieces (60) is a choice, not adaptive.

**What was attempted and failed.** (i) Verifying with one ball for the whole range $\varepsilon\in[10^{-24},10^{-7}]$: wide $\varepsilon$-balls make $s(x)$, $\lambda$ and the window positions blow up by the dependency problem of midpoint-radius arithmetic (e.g. lower bounds of $s$ negative, hence the three-valued inversion fails); hence the adaptive chain. (ii) The formula $s(x)=c_1(\arctan(\cdot)-x/\kappa)$ (the first form of Lemma 2.1) in balls: catastrophic cancellation, replaced by $g(y)/\tanh x-x$ with a series for small $y$. (iii) A $\lambda$-dependent form $dt/ds$ for the dormant mass: replaced by the $\lambda$-free ratio. (iv) The first cover below $10^{-19.6}$ gave NaN (the $z^2$ issue above).

### 12.4 Monotonicity in $\varepsilon$ (item 4)

**Question.** Does $\mathcal S(\varepsilon_1)$ imply $\mathcal S(\varepsilon)$ for all $\varepsilon\le\varepsilon_1$?

**Result: not proved in full; no counterexample; a proved $\varepsilon$-uniform envelope for the dormant part; and the interval cover of 12.3 replaces it on $[10^{-24},\varepsilon_{\rm top}]$.**

*Empirical.* On all 39 (target) grid scans of this note and 8.C (15 targets $K\le3$, 15 targets $K=4..6$, 9 planar targets; half-decade or decade grids from $10^{-3}$ down to $10^{-14}$) the pattern of $\mathcal S(\varepsilon_i)$ is monotone: once true it stays true at every smaller grid value. Together with the 45/45 + 45/45 refinement points this is the only evidence for the non-dormant constants. I found no counterexample, and no mechanism for one: every constant of $\mathcal S$ that depends on $\varepsilon$ does so through the closed forms of the unit (Lemma 2.1) with $O(\varepsilon)$ corrections, while the limit object does not depend on $\varepsilon$.

**Lemma 12.4 ($\varepsilon$-uniform envelope for the dormant coefficient; proved).** For every $\varepsilon\in(0,\varepsilon_{\rm top}]$ and every distance $s\ge25$ from the wall (in the dual coordinate $\eta$, i.e. $s=\lambda-|\eta|$, $\lambda\ge25$):
$$B_\varepsilon(s)\ \le\ \frac{4}{G\gamma^2m}\,\Big(1-\frac{\sigma_0^2}{s^2}\Big)^{-1}s^{-3},\qquad\sigma_0=\frac{a_{\max}}{2Gv_s}=20.$$
*Proof.* Lemma 2.2 gives the exact relation $\kappa\tanh x=\cot(\vartheta+x/\kappa)$ between the coordinate $x$ and the angle $\vartheta$; eliminating $x$ expresses $B_\varepsilon(s)\,s^3$ as the product of the constant $\rho_\varepsilon^{3/2}\varepsilon^{3/2}\lambda^3=(2v_s)^{3/2}\big(\tfrac{\pi a_{\max}}{4Gv_s}\big)^3$, which is **independent of $\varepsilon$**, and a function of an angle $t\in(0,\pi/2)$ of the form $\cos t\,(\sin t/t)^{-3}$ (times $1+O(\sigma_0^2/s^2)$ corrections collected in $(1-s_\varepsilon^2)^{-1}$). The Adamovi\'c-Mitrinovi\'c inequality $(\sin t/t)^3>\cos t$ bounds that function by $1$, and $s_\varepsilon\le\sigma_0/s$ gives $(1-s_\varepsilon^2)^{-1}\le(1-\sigma_0^2/s^2)^{-1}$. $\square$ (Numerics, Part U of `theory_t1b.md`: $\max_sB_\varepsilon s^3/\text{envelope}=0.395,\,0.973,\,0.9988,\,0.99995,\,\ldots$ for $\varepsilon=10^{-2},10^{-4},10^{-6},10^{-8},\dots$, always $\le1+6\cdot10^{-8}$, the overshoot being roundoff since the bound is asymptotically sharp; the grid minimum of $(\sin t)^3/(t^3\cos t)$ is $1.000000$.)

**Consequence (proved, for the envelope version of the dormant terms).** The envelope is $\varepsilon$-free as a function of the distance $s=\mathrm{dist}_l-D_l$ from the wall, and $\mathrm{dist}_l=\lambda(1-|u_l|)$ with $\lambda=c_u\varepsilon^{-1/2}(1+O(\varepsilon))\uparrow$ as $\varepsilon\downarrow0$. The dormant mass of a kernel is $\int w\,dt=\int B\,d\eta/|\dot\eta|$ over its approach to the wall, bounded by $\frac{1}{p}\int_{s_l}^\infty\frac{4}{G\gamma^2m}(1-\sigma_0^2/s^2)^{-1}s^{-3}ds\propto s_l^{-2}$; the drift terms are $\mu_c\,H\int w\,dt$-type quantities of the same form. All are decreasing functions of $s_l=\lambda\mu_l-D_l$, hence **non-increasing as $\varepsilon\downarrow0$ at fixed $D_l$**; (S1) and (S5) ($\max B<\theta$, $\sum B\le\Omega a_{\min}$) likewise. So, in the proof, the only $\varepsilon$-dependence of the dormant side is of the favourable sign; this is what Part U checks numerically.

**What is not proved.** The layer constants $T_1,\ A_{\rm tr},\ \mathrm{pre},\ \mathrm{Env},\ \kappa_*$ and the window half-widths $S^\pm_k$ ($\mathrm{pre}$ and $T_1$ are integrals of $d\eta/dx$ against the drive deficit $a_{\min}v_{lo}-W(\eta)$ over the unit's closed form) depend on $\varepsilon$ through $\kappa=\sqrt{(2v_s-\varepsilon)/\varepsilon}$ and $\lambda$. The exact relation of Lemma 2.2 expresses the distance as $s_\varepsilon(x)=c\big(g(y)/\tanh x-x\big)$, $g(y)=\arctan y/y$, $y=1/(\kappa\tanh x)$, so $s_\varepsilon\le s_0$ pointwise with a correction $O(\varepsilon/\tanh^3x)$ and $W_\varepsilon=W_0+(a/m)\varepsilon\tanh x$: i.e. every unit-level integral is $I_0+O(\varepsilon)$ with an explicit coefficient. This gives *convergence* of each constant as $\varepsilon\to0$ with the sign of the first correction computable, but a clean monotone-in-$\varepsilon$ statement would need the sign of $\partial_\varepsilon$ of an integral of a ratio of two such expressions on the whole range of $x$, including the region $x\to0$ where $\tanh^{-3}x$ is not integrable; I did not complete it. The lower end (below the cover) is therefore: convergence of the constants to their $\varepsilon\to0$ values (explicit $O(\varepsilon^{1/2})$ per the unit's closed forms) plus the Lemma 12.4 envelope; the ball cover of 12.3 reaches $10^{-24}$; the extension to $(0,10^{-24}]$ is **not written**.

**Why the half-decade grid + refinement is not a proof, and what replaces it.** Exactly this missing monotonicity; replaced by the ball cover (12.3) for $K\le3$, $n=1$.
